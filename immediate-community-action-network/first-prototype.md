# First Prototype

Build a small Santa Cruz County demonstration with two entry points:

1. **Available now:** independent or low-barrier actions with explicit permission and safety notes.
2. **Scheduled opportunities:** church, nonprofit, civic, and neighborhood activities with stated enrollment requirements.

Start with 10–20 records. Test browsing without an account, time-based search, clear requirements, source links, verification dates, correction reporting, and printable/mobile presentation. Do not claim complete coverage. Measure whether people can understand an opportunity quickly and avoid unnecessary travel, cost, or rejection.

Potential future integrations include organization widgets, browser sharing, calendar export, public kiosks, SMS/voice access, and APIs. Each integration should preserve source attribution, user privacy, and the distinction between independently accessible actions and organization-controlled activities.
