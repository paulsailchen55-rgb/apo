# Speaking Order

Speaking order is a procedural variable, not a guaranteed strategic choice.

The system should check the current meeting rules and explain:

- Whether speakers are called by agenda order, registration order, sign-up order, random selection, or chair/staff direction.
- Whether a speaker can request a position.
- Whether the user can choose general comment versus a scheduled agenda item.
- Whether the order is known in advance or only determined at the meeting.
- What legitimate preparation options exist if the user speaks early, late, or unexpectedly.

The system may discuss clarity, preparation, timing, and audience context. It must not advise deception, disruption, harassment, or interference with other participants.
