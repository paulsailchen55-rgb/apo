# Live Speech versus Playback Detection

Possible signals to investigate:
- Acoustic artifacts from speakers and microphones.
- Echo, reverberation, and room characteristics.
- Compression and playback artifacts.
- Timing and interaction patterns.
- Device or application metadata.
- Explicit user labels or controls.

Limitations:
- A microphone captures sound, not the complete history of sound production.
- High-quality playback may resemble live speech.
- Live speech may pass through microphones, headphones, conferencing systems, or other processing.
- The system should support an unknown or uncertain classification.
