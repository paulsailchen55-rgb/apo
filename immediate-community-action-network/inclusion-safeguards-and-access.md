# Inclusion, Safeguards, and Access

The project distinguishes concrete safety requirements from broad exclusionary labels. A listing should explain whether a restriction comes from law, safeguarding, insurance, training, supervision, or organizational policy.

The system should ask:

- What specific risk or responsibility requires the restriction?
- Is the restriction legally required or locally chosen?
- Is supervision or training an alternative?
- Is another task available immediately without that restriction?
- Can the person browse information and contribute without exposing sensitive personal details?

The platform must not help users evade legitimate safeguards involving children, vulnerable adults, private data, controlled environments, or specialized work. It should also not publish private identities, criminal-history claims, medical information, or informal stigmatizing classifications.

Accessibility includes people without an address, government ID, phone, stable internet, transportation, or predictable free time. The interface should support public computers, print, SMS/voice pathways where feasible, and clear offline instructions.
