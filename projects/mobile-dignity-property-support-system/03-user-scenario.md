# Step 3 — User Scenario

## Illustrative end-to-end scenario

1. **Initial connection:** A person is referred by a nonprofit, library, outreach worker, or service provider and receives a push-and-ride cargo trike through a lending or support program.
2. **Orientation and registration:** The person receives basic safety instruction, maintenance information, a program contact method, and a visible vehicle identifier. Personal belongings may be inventoried only with consent and appropriate privacy protections.
3. **Daily use:** The person pushes the trike while walking and uses the fold-out seat and pedal/ride function when longer travel makes that useful. Cargo areas protect clothing, documents, tools, food, and other belongings.
4. **Transit and appointments:** Before boarding a bus or entering a location where the trike cannot go, the person places the trike and belongings in an available secure storage pod, using a reservation or access process where possible.
5. **Return and retrieval:** After the appointment, work shift, or service visit, the person retrieves the stored property and continues traveling with the trike.
6. **Displacement or recovery event:** If the person is forced to relocate, loses access to a storage site, or the trike is found or removed, the program identifier supports a desired recovery process. Staff or authorized partners attempt to protect the belongings, document what occurred, and contact the person or their designated representative. Cooperation from public agencies or third parties must be established in advance and cannot be assumed.
7. **Continuing support:** The person can request repairs, replacement parts, storage assistance, referrals, or a change in equipment when circumstances change. Participation should not automatically depend on permanent housing or a fixed location.

## Scenario boundaries

This is an illustrative service pathway, not a claim that the program currently exists or that any agency will provide cooperation. Legal authority, consent, accessibility, safety, privacy, insurance, and local operating rules require separate investigation.
