# Research Question

Can an AI assistant identify whether incoming speech is being spoken live by a person or is playback of an earlier recording, and how can it communicate uncertainty responsibly?

The project should distinguish between:
- What is acoustically detectable.
- What can be inferred from device metadata.
- What the user explicitly labels.
- What cannot be established from the audio alone.

The objective is not necessarily to reject recorded audio. Source labeling could improve transcription, note organization, provenance tracking, and conversational context.
