# Government Authority / Committee / Commission Framework

## Status

Research framework; not a definitive organizational chart.

## Questions

The work explores how authority is divided among:
- federal committees and agencies
- state commissions and committees
- county boards and departments
- city councils and commissions
- administrative staff
- public finance and Ways and Means functions

The practical goal is to determine **who can answer, decide, review, fund, regulate, or route a question**.

## Method

For each civic issue:
1. identify the decision or action sought
2. identify the legal authority
3. identify the implementing agency
4. identify advisory/review bodies
5. identify public participation points
6. identify records and deadlines
7. preserve uncertainty where authority is unclear

## Important distinction

This research should not infer that a metaphorical structure is the actual structure of government.
