# Difficult Questions / Research Queue

## Transmission

1. Where does intended housing capacity first diverge from final housing output?
2. Which interfaces introduce measurable delay, cost, or loss?
3. Which frictions are intentional safeguards and which are unintended transaction costs?
4. Which distortions are caused by interactions rather than individual rules?
5. Where is friction merely relocated after reform?

## Feasibility

6. What is the marginal effect of an additional affordability obligation on project feasibility?
7. How does the effect differ by land cost, project type, density, financing, and market conditions?
8. How many affordable units are produced under alternative requirements, and how many total units are produced?
9. What happens to projects that become infeasible?

## Stability

10. What proportion of nominally affordable units achieve successful lease-up?
11. What proportion remain occupied over defined periods?
12. What causes loss of housing after initial placement?
13. Which administrative events interrupt otherwise stable housing?

## Provider participation

14. What transaction costs do landlords/providers experience under different programs?
15. Which program features affect willingness to participate?
16. How do inspection, rent review, documentation, payment timing, and recertification affect participation?

## Alternative systems

17. Can public land, long-term leases, resident ownership, cost-based rents, or shared-equity structures reduce recurring transaction costs?
18. Can a housing asset generate operating resources that reduce dependence on recurring subsidies?
19. What would a housing system look like if assistance followed the household without repeatedly rebuilding eligibility machinery?

## Historical test

20. Across successive Housing Element cycles, did reforms remove friction or create new interfaces?
21. Can the transmission model be measured against historical documents rather than used only as metaphor?
