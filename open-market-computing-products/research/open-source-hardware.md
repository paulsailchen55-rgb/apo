# Open-Source Hardware

## Research question

What existing open-hardware products and business models demonstrate that commercially obtainable computing hardware can be intentionally designed for modification and independent development?

## Questions

- Which products publish schematics?
- Which publish PCB designs?
- Which provide open firmware?
- Which allow firmware replacement?
- Which have proprietary components?
- How do they make money?
- What support models exist?
- What can a large established manufacturer learn from these examples?
