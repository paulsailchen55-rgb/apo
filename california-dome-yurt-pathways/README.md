# California Adaptive Dome & Yurt Housing Pathways

## Purpose

Help people who have already chosen dome- or yurt-based housing navigate California's complex property, zoning, permitting, engineering, accessibility, and development systems as easily as possible.

The project honors relevant knowledge from past generations while creating practical pathways for present and future generations.

## Initial Scope

- California-first; begin with Santa Cruz County.
- Existing-house replacement.
- New construction on vacant or newly zoned property.
- Accessory dwelling units and small structures.
- Dome and yurt communities.
- Nonprofit, cooperative, family, and individual development.
- AI-assisted guidance, databases, websites, printable instructions, and human contact pathways.

## Core Question

“You have chosen a dome or yurt. What do you need to do next, and how can we make each step easier?”

## Evidence Categories

- KNOWN
- CALCULATED
- TESTED
- PROPOSED
- SPECULATIVE
- UNKNOWN

This project does not attempt to persuade people to choose domes or yurts. It supports people who have already made that choice while clearly identifying requirements and uncertainties.
