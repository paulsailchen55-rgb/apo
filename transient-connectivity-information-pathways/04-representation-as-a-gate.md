# 04 — Representation as a Gate

Words, mathematics, images, theories, AI systems, and physical artifacts can be treated as interfaces through which representations move between states.

## Working proposition

> A representation is a boundary through which information passes between states of a system, and every representation can preserve, transform, compress, distort, or generate information.

The gate is not inherently good or bad. It is a transformation boundary.

A general chain is:

SOURCE STATE → OBSERVATION → REPRESENTATION → TRANSMISSION → INTERPRETATION → ACTION → NEW STATE

A boundary may also permit two-way exchange:

Q_in(t) and Q_out(t)

for material, energy, or other quantities. Informational exchange requires a defined channel and coding scheme.

## AI

AI is particularly relevant because it operates directly on representations. It can receive a representation, transform it, and produce another representation.

The project does not require treating AI as an ultimate observer. AI can instead be studied as one more transformation layer in an information pathway.

## Representational distance

A future research concept is a fidelity measure F(R0,Rn) between an originating representation R0 and a later representation Rn.

A possible distortion measure is:

D(R0,Rn) = 1 - F(R0,Rn)

The correct definition of F remains an open question.
