# Meaningful Housing Choice

Meaningful choice is more than being offered whatever unit is currently available. Relevant dimensions may include number and variety of options, affordability, location, accessibility, transportation, safety, privacy, rules, social environment, and the ability to decline without losing future assistance.

Questions for research:

- What information must be provided for informed choice?
- How much time is needed to evaluate an offer?
- Can people compare options across providers or jurisdictions?
- What protections exist against coercive or take-it-or-leave-it placement?
- How should personal preferences and lived knowledge be incorporated?
