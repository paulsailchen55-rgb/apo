# Provenance and Conversation History

Each addition should identify its source and preserve the difference between what was experienced, what was proposed, and what has been verified. These questions are documentation prompts, not requirements for perfect certainty.

## Ten questions for further inquiry
1. Where did this material originate: current conversation, separate chat, recording, field note, research, or later interpretation?
2. What is the approximate date and setting?
3. Is the material an observation, interpretation, hypothesis, proposal, or verified claim?
4. What context is necessary for another reader to understand it fairly?
5. Which sources, documents, or people support or complicate it?
6. What uncertainty or missing information should be recorded?
7. Has the material been revised, summarized, or moved into another document?
8. Which related files should link back to this source?
9. How should the material be cited without exposing unnecessary personal information?
10. What should remain unchanged so the original record is not silently rewritten?
