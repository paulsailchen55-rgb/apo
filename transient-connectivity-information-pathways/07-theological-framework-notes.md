# 07 — Theological and Philosophical Framework Notes

This file records the theological layer that informed the model. It does not present theology as scientific evidence.

## Transcendence and ineffability

Terms considered during the conversation include ineffable, transcendent, unknowable, incomprehensible, unrepresentable, and apophatic.

The important structural idea is that finite language may point toward something it cannot completely contain.

## Image and source

The central distinction is:

IMAGE ≠ SOURCE

The conversation applied this to the user's understanding of the "image of God": an image can participate in representation without being identical to the reality represented.

The information-theory parallel is:

MODEL ≠ MODELED REALITY

## Proposed hierarchy

GREATER / TRANSCENDENT REALITY
↓
CREATION
↓
HUMAN BEINGS
↓
HUMAN CONCEPTUAL MODELS
↓
WORDS, MATHEMATICS, IMAGES, THEORIES, AI, ARTIFACTS

A reverse or downward branch was then proposed as a darkened state.

## Divine mediation

The user's biblical framework proposes that divine reach is not stopped by the apparent lowest boundary.

The formal analogue is:

LOCAL STATE + GREATER-SYSTEM COUPLING → POSSIBLE RESTORATION

This does not establish the theological premise scientifically.

## Prayer, praise, and transformation

The conversation considered whether directing a state toward God through prayer or praise could be compared with an observation or relational interaction that changes the state.

This remains a theological proposition. It should not be presented as a consequence of quantum mechanics.

## Universe as self-representation

Another philosophical proposition is that the universe contains temporary localized structures capable of representing and modeling the larger system of which they are parts.

A neutral formulation is:

> A physical system can contain localized structures capable of modeling the larger system of which they are temporary parts.

That provides a bridge to information theory and AI without equating the universe with God.
