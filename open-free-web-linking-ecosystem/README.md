# Open & Free Web Linking Ecosystem

**Status:** PROPOSED / CONCEPTUAL

## Purpose

Explore an open, affordable, and portable way for people to build an online presence by connecting free website hosts, free website builders, AI-assisted creation tools, and user-controlled custom links.

The goal is not to recreate a centralized social-media platform. The goal is to help people assemble an online presence from independent services while retaining readable links, portability, and alternatives to mandatory subscriptions and advertising.

## Core Model

A person can:

1. Create or generate a webpage using a free or open tool.
2. Publish it through a free hosting provider such as GitHub Pages, Netlify, Neocities, or Cloudflare Pages.
3. Connect the page to a custom redirect or link-routing system.
4. Use a memorable public link with a meaningful path, such as `/about`, `/projects`, or `/contact`.
5. Change the underlying host later while preserving the public-facing link, where the routing service supports it.

## 80/20 Design Objective

**PROPOSED PRINCIPLE:** Explore an internet ecosystem in which approximately 80% of basic publishing, linking, and participation needs can be met through open, free, community-supported, or user-controlled infrastructure, while paid and advertising-supported services remain optional rather than unavoidable.

The 80/20 figure is a design aspiration, not a measured current statistic or a prediction.

## Guiding Values

- Portability over lock-in
- Open standards over proprietary dependence
- Free access where realistically sustainable
- Clear disclosure of hosting, domain, bandwidth, and maintenance costs
- Simple explanations for people with limited money, time, or technical experience
- Independent identity without requiring social-media participation
- No deletion, merger, or centralization of separate projects without explicit approval

## Initial Service Categories

- Free static hosting
- Free website builders and HTML generators
- AI-assisted creation tools
- Custom redirects and link routing
- Custom domains and DNS
- Documentation, templates, and migration guides

## Scope Boundary

This project documents possibilities and tests service combinations. It does not assume that any provider will remain free, available, ad-free, privacy-preserving, or operational indefinitely.
