# AI-Mediated Influence Threat Model

## Archive status

This folder preserves a two-response conversation archive concerning a proposed AI security threat model: human bad actors manipulating AI-mediated information and communication pathways in order to influence other humans, including through alteration, omission, delay, disruption, context manipulation, or covert use of compromised systems.

This is an **APO archive**, not a validated security finding or established factual claim.

## Contents

- [response-1.md](response-1.md) — first assistant response, preserved verbatim.
- [response-2.md](response-2.md) — second assistant response, preserved verbatim.

## Provenance

The two response files are intended to preserve the assistant's wording exactly as it appeared in the conversation at the time of archival. They include the assistant's own qualifications and distinctions.

## Working classification

- **KNOWN:** The conversation exists and these responses were produced.
- **PROPOSED:** AI-mediated influence as a security/threat-model concept.
- **UNRESOLVED:** What technical mechanisms could produce each proposed form of manipulation, how they could be detected, and what evidence would distinguish malicious manipulation from ordinary AI error, outage, or system behavior.
