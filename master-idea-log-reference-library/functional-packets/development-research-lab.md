# Development Research Lab — Experimental Ideas

Status: exploratory. These ideas are not claims of technical feasibility.

Research method:
Idea → existing example → engineering review → legal/regulatory review → economics → environmental effects → possible pilot.

Experiments/questions:
- Pillar-based modular structural platforms.
- Movable residential or commercial modules.
- Road/rail-compatible modular movement.
- Prepared parking/transfer lots as temporary capacity.
- Productive Vertical Commons combining housing, common space, green space, accessibility, and productive uses.
- Microleasing of small spaces/capacity.
- Temporary agricultural or productive urban layers.
- Small-scale animal uses where appropriate and lawful.
- Building passports and open building information.
- Material concentration for future recovery.
- Long-life infrastructure with replaceable building components.

For every experiment, preserve the distinction between a conceptual sketch and a tested design. Structural/seismic, fire, accessibility, utility, transportation, title/ownership, insurance, financing, environmental, maintenance, and code questions remain open until qualified review.
