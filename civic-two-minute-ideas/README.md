# Civic Two-Minute Ideas

## Purpose

An adaptive, AI-assisted civic communication system that helps people separate ideas from the APO archive, verify current government procedures and project sources, and prepare clear public comments or meeting requests.

The two-minute speech is a universal preparation target, not a universal legal limit. The system must check the rules for the specific jurisdiction, meeting, agenda item, and participation mode.

## Two implementation sides

1. **AI/workflow side:** prompts, source retrieval, document comparison, idea separation, evidence handling, agenda analysis, speech generation, communication-fit guidance, and strategy updates. This may be developed through AI-assisted or “vibe coding” workflows.
2. **App/prototype side:** a downloadable, self-contained HTML generator that can run as a practical user interface, accept project and official source documents, and produce structured outputs.

Both sides should use a shared data model.

## Core principles

- Current-source awareness: retrieve and record the latest available official materials.
- Source provenance: show which documents were used and when they were checked.
- Evidence distinctions: preserve KNOWN, CALCULATED, TESTED, PROPOSED, SPECULATIVE, and UNKNOWN categories.
- Human control: the user reviews and approves speech content.
- Procedural compliance: artistic and strategic options must remain within applicable rules.
- Accessibility: plain-language explanations and low-barrier participation guidance.
- Public context: account for officials, staff, and members of the public who may watch recordings.
- No manipulation: communication optimization should improve clarity, respect, and audience fit rather than exploit psychological vulnerabilities.
- Preserve uncertainty and never imply that AI has verified a source it did not actually check.

## Initial case study

Santa Cruz City and Santa Cruz County, California. The system should later accept official source documents from other cities, counties, states, agencies, and meeting bodies.

## Status

Proposed archive/project concept. Architecture, source rules, and prototypes remain under development.
