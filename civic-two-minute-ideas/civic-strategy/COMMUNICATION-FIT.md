# Communication Fit

This module adapts delivery to the audience and participation mode without claiming to predict or control an individual's psychology.

Possible considerations:

- Plain-language versus technical explanation
- Personal experience versus documented evidence
- Voice pacing and pauses
- Visual support for video or in-person use
- Accessibility and captioning needs
- Whether a handout or follow-up note would help
- A concise opening, clear request, and respectful closing

Suggestions should be framed as options with limitations, not guaranteed persuasion techniques.
