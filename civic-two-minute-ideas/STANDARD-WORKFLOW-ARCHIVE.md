# Standard Workflow Archive — Civic Communication Lab

**Status:** ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT ARCHIVE
**Project:** Civic Communication Lab / Civic Two-Minute Ideas
**Initial case study:** Santa Cruz City and County, California

## Purpose

This document records how the project is being developed, not only the resulting design. It preserves decisions, distinctions, open questions, corrections, evidence classifications, and the relationship between each development stage and the APO documents updated.

## Development method

1. State the current stage and its questions.
2. Receive the project initiator's answers and additional observations.
3. Separate confirmed decisions from proposals, assumptions, and unknowns.
4. Identify which existing document should be updated or whether a new document is required.
5. Update the relevant APO file without deleting or silently merging existing material.
6. Record the change, evidence status, and remaining questions.
7. Continue to the next stage only after the current stage is sufficiently documented.

## Development map

### Stage 1 — Foundation

- Primary users: all defined groups, including beginners, idea holders, and experienced civic participants seeking speed.
- Entry methods: existing APO project, typed or spoken idea, uploaded document or photograph, or combinations.
- AI assistance: AI-first drafting with review, collaborative development, and user-first drafting with later AI assistance.
- Outcomes: concise speech, printable document, practice, timing variants, notes, and follow-up preparation, with optional steps.
- Documents: `STAGE-1-FOUNDATION.md`, `ARCHITECTURE.md`, and `DESIGN-PARAMETERS.md`.

### Stage 2 — Entry and intake

Questions include onboarding order, minimum intake information, idea separation, and how human-first access appears at the beginning.

New design input: human assistance should be offered before digital tools as a primary pathway. This is documented in `HUMAN-FIRST-ACCESS.md` and should be integrated into the intake design.

### Stages 3–8 — To be developed

The remaining stages will be defined incrementally. Likely areas include source verification, jurisdiction and agenda analysis, communication generation, practice and accessibility, HTML implementation, and testing/hand-off. These labels remain provisional until discussed and confirmed.

## Evidence labels

- **KNOWN:** directly established by an authoritative source or confirmed project decision.
- **CALCULATED:** derived through an explicit calculation.
- **TESTED:** implemented and evaluated with recorded results.
- **PROPOSED:** design suggestion or user-approved direction not yet implemented.
- **SPECULATIVE:** exploratory possibility requiring investigation.
- **UNKNOWN:** not yet established.

## Archival requirements

Each update should preserve:

- Date and stage.
- User decisions and meaningful wording distinctions.
- Document paths changed or created.
- Evidence classifications.
- Unresolved questions and limitations.
- Any correction to earlier assumptions.
- Whether a feature is core, optional, related, or experimental.

## Current distinction to preserve

The system is not intended to push every person toward AI. It should help people remain connected with humans and provide verified local human-contact options first, followed by optional web, HTML, AI, practice, printing, and Labs pathways.
