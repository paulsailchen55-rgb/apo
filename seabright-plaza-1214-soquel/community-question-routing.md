# 1214 Soquel Avenue / Seabright Plaza — Community Question Routing

## Status

Supplement to the existing site-specific archive.

## Research posture

Questions should be presented as questions derived from community concerns, not as assumed facts.

Potential topics:
- project/preapplication status
- SB 330 applicability/status
- zoning and allowed height/density
- corridor planning history
- traffic
- parking
- environmental review
- trees
- stormwater
- fire/life safety
- utilities
- loading
- existing businesses
- what is legally required versus what is voluntarily proposed
- protections or transition options for existing businesses

## Process

For each question:
1. Identify the responsible agency or source.
2. Find the governing document or public record.
3. Record the date.
4. Separate verified fact from reported concern.
5. Preserve disagreements.
6. Avoid converting a community question into an accusation.

## Communication goal

Provide officials and community participants with a narrow, legible set of questions that can be answered without requiring them to process an enormous packet.
