## Reading guidance

The folders and documents in this repository have different statuses. Some contain observations or questions, some contain speculative research concepts, and some may contain documented research or public-process material. Readers should review the stated scope and evidence limits of each entry rather than assuming every archived idea is established or validated.

The archive is designed to be accessible through direct links and ordinary search engines without requiring social-media participation.
