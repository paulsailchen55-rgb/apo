# Housing Placement and Market Structure

## Ten questions
1. How are housing options identified and offered?
2. Who decides which units a person can see?
3. How does limited supply affect practical choice?
4. What information is provided about landlords, rules, costs, and conditions?
5. Can applicants compare offers from different providers?
6. How do vouchers, waitlists, and eligibility rules affect mobility?
7. What happens when an offered unit is unsuitable?
8. Are people able to appeal or request review?
9. How might ownership concentration affect available choices?
10. What public data would help evaluate fairness and access?
