# Firmware and Bootloader Access

## Research question

What minimum level of firmware and bootloader access is necessary for a computing product to qualify as genuinely open?

## Questions

- Can the bootloader be unlocked?
- Can firmware be replaced?
- Can firmware be modified?
- Are signing keys required?
- Can owners restore the original software?
- Are proprietary binary components permitted?
- How should security-sensitive components be handled?
