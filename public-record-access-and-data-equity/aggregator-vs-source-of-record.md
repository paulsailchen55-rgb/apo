# Aggregator vs. Source of Record

A commercial property-data service may combine assessor, recorder, tax, permit, listing, market, and other information into a convenient interface. That convenience can be useful, but aggregation can introduce subscription requirements, stale data, normalization/interpretation, omitted fields, conflicting updates, or unclear provenance.

Preferred civic-research chain:

Private/secondary clue -> identify alleged underlying record -> locate originating agency or authoritative source -> open or request the source -> record exact date/status -> preserve the secondary clue separately.

A commercial profile reporting parcel size, year built, ownership, or land use should therefore be treated as a lead until the applicable public record is checked.

Design rule: do not make the aggregator the authority merely because it is easier to search.
