# Santa Cruz County Housing: Subsidy Stack & Feasibility

## Status
**PROPOSED / RESEARCH IN PROGRESS**

This folder documents a systems-level housing feasibility question:

> How do land, zoning, density, inclusionary requirements, impact fees, infrastructure, financing, subsidies, construction costs, approval time, and market conditions interact to determine whether housing actually gets built?

This is not a recommendation for a particular political outcome. It is a framework for testing whether the combined requirements placed on a project are economically feasible and whether the resulting system produces the intended number of affordable homes.

## Core finding to date

Santa Cruz County's 2026 inclusionary feasibility work makes the subsidy-stack question concrete. The County's consultant, Keyser Marston Associates (KMA), evaluated inclusionary requirements together with development assumptions and density-bonus scenarios. The study reports that a 12% low-income requirement can be supported by zoning-compliant rental development under its prototype assumptions.

The central research question therefore shifts from:

**"What inclusionary percentage should we choose?"**

to:

**"What complete combination of density, development standards, fees, financing, subsidies, market revenue, and affordability requirements produces the greatest feasible housing production?"**
