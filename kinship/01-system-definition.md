# KINSHIP System Definition

## Purpose
KINSHIP is a proposed family-scale habitat designed to remain habitable across changing environmental conditions by changing configuration, location, attachment, and protective envelope.

## Core modes
**Walk · Anchor · Level · Swim · Connect · Release · Repair**

These are conceptual states, not validated capabilities.

## Central requirement
> KINSHIP must be able to adapt to another mode easily enough to preserve its inhabitants and itself.

The system does not need to defeat every hazard in place. Where physically possible, it should transition from one survivable state to another.

## Life before identity
Basic physical protection should not depend on government ID, citizenship, social status, biometric identity, or an account. This does not mean every medical, communications, community, or administrative function must be anonymous.

## Capability layers
The concept is not necessarily an upgrade ladder. The same architecture may be implemented as:

**Dome structure → sensorized structure → autonomous structure → robotic structure → potentially intelligent habitat.**

Advanced intelligence is not a prerequisite for basic survival.

A useful hierarchy is passive protection → sensing → biological safety monitoring → autonomous protective actions → adaptive maintenance → cooperative intelligence → potentially embodied intelligence.

## Adaptive integrity
A habitat can remain successful even when its appearance, components, or configuration change as a consequence of surviving environmental events.

A damaged tree is a natural metaphor: bent, scarred, partially broken, or altered, yet still alive and functioning. The metaphor is not an engineering claim.

## Survival priorities
An unresolved central question is what KINSHIP protects first when it cannot save everything simultaneously. Candidates for evaluation include human life, breathable atmosphere, immediate hazard separation, essential structural integrity, transition capability, survival power, water, mobility, communications, and nonessential systems.

The related principle is:

> The habitat does not have to preserve every component. It has to preserve the continuity of habitation.

## Evidence status
Use **KNOWN, CALCULATED, TESTED, PROPOSED, SPECULATIVE, UNKNOWN** to distinguish evidence from hypotheses.

## Boundary
KINSHIP is not currently a certified building, boat, aircraft, autonomous robot, or guarantee of survival. No material is assumed universally fireproof, bulletproof, or indestructible.
