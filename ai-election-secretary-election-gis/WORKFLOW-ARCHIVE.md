# WORKFLOW ARCHIVE

## 2026-09-24 — Initial architecture

### Trigger
Search for an AI note-taking / secretary application led to a broader question: could a host/router receive raw material, create a brief, understand the task, and direct the work to the appropriate AI or tool?

### Architecture shift
The concept changed from selecting one "best AI" to designing an AI Secretary that orchestrates multiple specialized capabilities.

### Election use case
The architecture was connected to researching election decisions before a person makes them.

### Initial pipeline
DISCOVERY -> BRIEF -> EVIDENCE -> ADVERSARIAL RESEARCH -> SOURCE COMPARISON -> RELATIONAL MAP -> DECISION TREE -> HUMAN DECISION -> ARCHIVE

### Candidate model
Candidate dossiers replace simple biographies for deep research.

### GIS insight
Candidate information can be represented as layers over geographic and institutional terrain.

### Important distinction
VOTABLE BY ME != RELEVANT TO ME

### Three circles
MY BALLOT / MY GOVERNMENT / MY ENVIRONMENT

### Election-territory insight
Election consolidation documents reveal overlapping jurisdictions and electoral machinery. They are useful as GIS boundary/authority layers even when they do not decide the merits of the measures being placed on the ballot.

### Institutional-layer extension
The project expanded from Election GIS to Civic Institutional GIS.

New layers:
- geography
- authority
- funding
- people
- appointments
- organizations
- programs
- contracts
- property
- outcomes
- time

### Institutional trajectory
A static dossier is insufficient for some questions.

The system should support time slices and transitions so that documented institutional movement can be examined over time.

### Funding / capacity insight
Financial-flow research should distinguish revenue, expenses, assets, liabilities, net assets, restricted resources, contract reimbursements, local economic retention, outside spending, property infrastructure, and durable capacity.

The system should not infer extraction or misconduct from financial ratios alone.

### Institutional knowledge
Staff continuity is a possible systems variable. Research should examine whether turnover interrupts long-running regulatory, property, service, or administrative processes.

### Sustainable Service Capacity Review
Proposed analytical framework:

ACTUAL DOCUMENTED COST
+
REQUIRED SERVICE CAPACITY
+
STAFFING / PROPERTY / COMPLIANCE CAPACITY
vs.
AVAILABLE FUNDING

The purpose is to identify documented capacity gaps before service continuity is compromised.

### Global extension
The project was explicitly expanded to elections worldwide, including single-party systems and political systems where electoral events do not function like U.S. competitive elections.

### Safety / integrity requirement
The system must support human decision-making rather than make political choices for people.

### Evidence discipline
Use:
KNOWN
CALCULATED
TESTED
PROPOSED
SPECULATIVE
UNKNOWN

Add, where useful:
DOCUMENTED CLAIM
CONTESTED
UNVERIFIED
