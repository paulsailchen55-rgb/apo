# Right to Repair

## Research question

How does existing right-to-repair policy overlap with the proposed concept of Open Market Computing Products?

## Questions

- What rights do current repair laws provide?
- Do they reach firmware and software?
- Do they provide access to diagnostic software?
- Do they protect independent repair businesses?
- Where do they stop short of independent software development?
- What lessons can be carried into a broader open-computing framework?

## Distinction

Right to repair generally concerns maintaining or restoring a product.

The proposed open-market concept potentially goes further by asking whether consumers should have access to products intentionally designed for independent software and hardware development.
