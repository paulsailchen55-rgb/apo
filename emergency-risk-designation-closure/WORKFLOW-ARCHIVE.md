## 2026-09-24 — Initial archival decision

### User-originated concern

The user described hearing a woman at a Board of Supervisors meeting in support of a county suicide proclamation and developed a hypothetical emergency-resilience failure scenario.

The concern was not simply about suicide prevention. It concerned the possibility that a broad safety designation could create a repeating cycle:

**concern → designation → monitoring → data collection → extraction/inference → continued justification → further monitoring**

The user connected this to a broader theory that systems can become overly redundant and extractive in cyclic ways until the system itself becomes unstable or “crashes.”

### Important correction preserved

The initial conversation included the idea that people with alcohol or drug problems are “pretty much suicidal.”

For archival rigor, this is preserved as the user's hypothesis/concern rather than as a factual proposition.

The project explicitly does **not** treat substance use as synonymous with suicidal intent.

### Assistant refinement

The concept was refined into:

**mission creep through persistent risk labeling and data reuse**

and into a broader design principle:

> Every intervention needs a defined purpose, authority, scope, review point, and termination condition.

### Design sequence identified

**Concern identified → assessment → appropriate intervention → reassessment → risk no longer substantiated → intervention terminates → unnecessary temporary data is deleted or segregated → no secondary use without independent authorization.**

### AI extension

The discussion identified a separate concern when AI is introduced:

**observation → classification → inference → retention → sharing → action**

Authorization for one stage should not automatically authorize the next.

### Archival placement

The project was placed in a new self-contained APO folder:

emergency-risk-designation-closure/

This was selected rather than modifying an existing project because the concept overlaps several existing APO projects but has its own central research question: **bounded emergency authority and genuine closure.**
