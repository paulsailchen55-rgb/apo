# Design Parameters

## Stage 1 — User and workflow flexibility

- Support new civic participants, people organizing an idea, and experienced participants seeking speed.
- Offer multiple entry methods: existing APO project, typed idea, spoken idea, document upload, photograph upload, or combinations.
- Support three AI assistance modes: AI draft then review, collaborative development, and user-authored draft followed by AI assistance.
- Make outcomes optional: concise speech, printable document, practice, short version, two-minute version, expanded version, supporting notes, and follow-up preparation.
- Preserve original user input and distinguish it from AI-generated organization, interpretation, and suggestions.
- Allow users to revisit steps, change assistance modes, skip optional steps, or stop after a draft.
- Use plain-language guidance without assuming prior civic knowledge, literacy level, technical skill, or available time.

## General design constraints

- Two minutes is a preparation target, not an assumed legal limit.
- Support 30-second, 2-minute, 5-minute, and meeting-length versions.
- Check current rules and record source date, URL, title, revision, and section.
- Treat phone, video, in-person, written, and scheduled meeting modes separately.
- Keep the basic path functional; place experimental artistic concepts in Labs.
- Check every Lab concept for rule compliance, accessibility, safety, privacy, and practicality.
- Do not claim communication tactics guarantee a psychological response.
- Trace claims to sources, user input, calculations, or clearly labeled proposals.
- Do not require recording, public sharing, or submission for use of the basic workflow.
- Clearly distinguish locally supplied sources from externally verified sources.
