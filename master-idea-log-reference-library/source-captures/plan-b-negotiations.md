# Plan B Negotiations with Workbench — Source Capture

Source: user-provided one-page document, September 2026.

1. Lower building to fewer than 4 stories; require trees/landscaping and set back from sidewalk.
2. Build significantly more ground-level commercial space (at least 3x more = 15,000 ft2); provide coordination with City for relocation compensation for shuttered businesses; provide first right of refusal for those businesses once project completed and/or assure long-term lease options.
3. Do not start demolition after entitlement so businesses are not kicked early and to limit long-term vacancy before construction.
4. Require fully vetted traffic/parking plan during construction.
5. Make the project a mix of affordable units to market-rate units to ensure a combination of income levels/diversity.
6. Alternative location: spread same number of units over multiple sites so housing complexes have fewer stories, less intrusive, and fit into neighborhood. Source cites Riverwalk Apt on Lindberg St, north of Water, west side of river, as a model to examine.

These are preserved as community negotiation proposals, not statements of current law or project requirements.
