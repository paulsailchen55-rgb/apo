# Anonymization and Provenance Notes

## What is preserved

- The fact that an AI-assisted public-interest proposal was developed.
- The fact that real-world outreach occurred.
- The broad process responses observed.
- The STUR + Closure analysis.
- The distinction between observation and interpretation.

## What is intentionally not preserved

- Private email addresses.
- Personal names not necessary to understand the research.
- Account identifiers.
- Ticket/case numbers.
- Unnecessary verbatim messages.
- Claims about motive, quality, legality, competence, or institutional intent.

## Provenance rule

The archive should preserve the chain:

**HUMAN OBSERVATION → AI-ASSISTED INTERPRETATION → REAL-WORLD ACTION → OBSERVED RESPONSE → INTERPRETATION → DESIGN QUESTION**

An assistant-generated interpretation must remain identifiable as interpretation.

## Current limitation

This is an anonymized secondary record, not a complete evidentiary record. Any future publication making claims about external organizations should use independently verified primary sources.
