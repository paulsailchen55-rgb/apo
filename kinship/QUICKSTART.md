# KINSHIP Quickstart

## What is it?

A family-scale habitat intended to preserve continuity of habitation by changing modes as environmental conditions change.

## Core vocabulary

Walk · Anchor · Level · Swim · Connect · Release · Repair

## Evidence rule

Every significant technical statement should eventually be labeled:

- KNOWN
- CALCULATED
- TESTED
- PROPOSED
- SPECULATIVE
- UNKNOWN

## Where to work

Use the numbered system documents for individual engineering domains. Use OPEN-QUESTIONS.md for unresolved issues and ROADMAP.md for sequencing.

This document is a navigation aid, not a technical specification.
