# Design Parameters

- Make the pathway understandable without prior legal or technical knowledge.
- Preserve links to authoritative sources and dates checked.
- Provide plain-language and detailed versions.
- Support phone, web, printable, and accessibility-friendly formats.
- Distinguish legal requirements from recommendations and project preferences.
- Do not imply that a dome or yurt is automatically approved or safe on every property.
- Support tiny dwellings, large homes, and connected communities.
- Preserve user choice while identifying constraints.
