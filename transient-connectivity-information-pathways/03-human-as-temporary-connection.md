# 03 — Human Beings as Temporary Connections

The conversation developed a philosophical image of human life as a temporary, localized event within a reality vastly greater than the human frame.

The phrase "one breath" is a metaphor for scale and temporality, not a proposed physical unit.

## Working proposition

> A finite system may participate in a larger system without being capable of completely representing that larger system.

A human being can be understood as:

- a temporary organized structure;
- an observer;
- a participant;
- a source of disturbances;
- a receiver of disturbances;
- a producer and interpreter of representations.

## Image and source

A representation is not identical to what it represents.

This can be stated as:

MODEL ≠ MODELED REALITY

MAP ≠ TERRITORY

IMAGE ≠ SOURCE

The theological discussion connected this distinction to the concept of humanity as an "image" without equating the image with the reality represented.

## Human-to-AI chain

A possible information chain is:

REALITY → EXPERIENCE → HUMAN MODEL → REPRESENTATION → AI TRANSFORMATION → NEW REPRESENTATION → HUMAN INTERPRETATION

At each stage, information may be preserved, compressed, transformed, lost, or reconstructed.

The research problem is to determine what structure survives successive transformations.
