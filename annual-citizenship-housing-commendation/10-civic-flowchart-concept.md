# Civic Flowchart Concept

Objective: make government and community processes visible and understandable to ordinary residents.

Conceptual skeleton:

Need / crisis / homelessness
-> outreach or self-referral
-> assessment
-> eligibility determination
-> referral
-> shelter / treatment / probation / case management / other pathway
-> transitional or supportive services
-> housing opportunity
-> lease / placement
-> ongoing support
-> community participation

Possible starting points include unsheltered homelessness, shelter entry, law-enforcement contact, court involvement, probation, substance-use services, behavioral-health services, family/community referral, housing waitlist, nonprofit outreach, and self-referral.

Where possible, identify human roles: outreach workers, caseworkers, clinicians, probation personnel, housing navigators, shelter staff, housing managers, public administrators, nonprofit workers, and community volunteers.

Purpose: civic literacy, not surveillance or case tracking.
