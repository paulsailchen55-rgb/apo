# KINSHIP Open Questions

This document collects unresolved questions rather than hiding them.

Initial questions include:

1. What must be protected first when KINSHIP cannot protect everything simultaneously?
2. What functions may be deliberately sacrificed to preserve life?
3. What geometry provides a useful balance among structural efficiency, manufacturability, habitability, and repair?
4. What lower-pod geometry provides adequate passive stability and damage tolerance?
5. What four-arm architecture can carry, stabilize, move, and manipulate the habitat?
6. How can anchoring and controlled release coexist?
7. How can protective skins deploy rapidly without creating new failure modes?
8. Which materials satisfy the required performance and end-of-life constraints?
9. What energy budget separates survival functions from mobility?
10. Which capabilities should remain manual even in highly automated versions?
