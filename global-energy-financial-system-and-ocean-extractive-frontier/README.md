# Global Energy, Financial Systems, and the Ocean as an Extractive Frontier

## Status

- **Project type:** APO concept and research archive
- **Status:** Proposed / exploratory
- **Origin:** Conversation archive, September 22, 2026
- **Evidence posture:** Separate documented observations, research questions, hypotheses, and items requiring verification

## Central Question

When a major geopolitical, energy, or financial shock occurs, who absorbs the cost, how is that cost distributed, and which institutional or technological alternatives could reduce disproportionate burdens on people with the fewest resources?

## Related Systems

- Fossil-fuel dependence and extraction
- Oil transportation chokepoints, especially the Strait of Hormuz
- Dollar-based oil trade and international finance
- Inflation and Federal Reserve interest-rate decisions
- Household exposure to energy, food, transportation, housing, and borrowing costs
- Ocean-resource extraction as a possible extension of extractive economic systems
- Ecological protection, regeneration, resilience, and systemic redesign

## Working Principle

A system may be constrained without being incapable of change. Research should distinguish physical limits from institutional choices, and should examine who receives benefits and who bears costs.

## Important Caution

This project does not assume that perpetual war, inevitable ocean extraction, or any single causal explanation is predetermined. These are questions and scenarios to be investigated with evidence.
