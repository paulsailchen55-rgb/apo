# Historical and Cultural Context

## Purpose

Investigate California Indigenous architectural traditions and other relevant historical structures as a way of honoring past generations and learning how people adapted housing to local environments.

## Research Standards

Document specific communities and regions, structure types and materials, environmental and cultural context, archaeological evidence, Indigenous sources and interpretations where available, and distinctions between established findings and modern hypotheses.

Do not generalize one architectural form or motivation to all Indigenous peoples. Claims about circular depressions, mounds, collapsed structures, sacred spaces, smoke, or burial practices must be separately sourced and labeled as KNOWN, SPECULATIVE, or UNKNOWN.
