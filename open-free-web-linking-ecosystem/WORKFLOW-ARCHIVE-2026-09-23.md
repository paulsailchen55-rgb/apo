# Workflow Archive — 2026-09-23

## Origin

The concept developed from discussing free website hosting, custom linkers, and ways to connect independently hosted pages so they can appear to visitors as one coherent online presence.

## User's Core Direction

Investigate an open and free platform model that links together free hosting services, free website builders, AI-assisted tools, and custom link routing. The intention is to provide practical alternatives to centralized, subscription-driven, and advertising-dependent social-media ecosystems.

## Preserved Principle

The user described an aspirational 80/20 model: ideally, roughly 80% of basic internet publishing and participation infrastructure would be open/free or broadly accessible, while only a smaller portion would depend on subscriptions and/or advertising. This is recorded as a proposed value and design objective, not as an established measurement.

## Decisions Recorded

- Create a standalone APO project rather than modifying the root README.
- Preserve the project as a concept that can be expanded through research and testing.
- Treat GitHub Pages, Netlify, Neocities, and Cloudflare Pages as candidate hosting categories, not guaranteed permanent solutions.
- Include AI-assisted and free website-building tools as a creation layer, while distinguishing them from hosting and routing.
- Investigate readable custom paths and redirect systems that can preserve a public link while allowing the destination host to change.
- Document costs, limitations, privacy, portability, and provider dependency honestly.

## Current Classification

- KNOWN: Free hosting and website-building services exist in multiple forms.
- PROPOSED: A coordinated ecosystem of independent free services with custom routing.
- SPECULATIVE: A broad 80/20 internet ecosystem becoming practical at scale.
- UNKNOWN: Long-term provider stability, pricing, policy changes, portability, and sustainable operating costs.

## Next Research Direction

Build a comparison matrix of hosting providers, website builders, custom-domain options, redirect tools, privacy practices, account requirements, limits, and migration paths. Then design a small proof of concept using one static page and one editable custom link.
