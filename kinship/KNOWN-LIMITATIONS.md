# KINSHIP Known Limitations

KINSHIP is presently a conceptual architecture and research archive.

It does not currently establish:

- certified structural performance
- certified fire resistance
- certified ballistic or impact resistance
- certified vessel stability or seaworthiness
- certified aircraft performance
- certified autonomous operation
- guaranteed survival in any hazard
- validated self-righting performance
- validated four-arm mobility
- validated aquatic propulsion
- validated transforming armor
- validated materials proposed in conversation

Unvalidated ideas remain proposals, speculation, or unknowns.
