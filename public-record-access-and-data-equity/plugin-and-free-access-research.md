The available app/plugin search found a Zillow app, described around real-estate listings, maps, financing, neighborhood information, and market data. No dedicated Redfin connector or Santa Cruz assessor/recorder connector was identified in that directory search.

This is limited to the available ChatGPT app/plugin directory at the time checked. It does not establish that no connector exists elsewhere or that a government agency lacks an API.

Connector availability is a tooling question, not authority for a property fact.

Future research should catalog official GIS/assessor/recorder/permit endpoints, public APIs/downloads, public-record request procedures, free nonprofit/library resources, commercial aggregators, and what each layer can verify.
