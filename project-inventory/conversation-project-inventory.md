## A. Projects or substantial project bodies

1. **Civic Flow Chart** — project / civic-process infrastructure concept.
   Purpose: make public procedures visible, navigable, reversible, time-aware, responsibility-aware, and understandable to residents and officials, including emergency use. Core STUR + Closure model; friction, clocks, authority, cost, evidence, feedback, backtracking, and end-of-life.
   Archive status: represented in `civic-flow-chart/` and also developed in the separate `civic-flow-chart-project` repository.

2. **California Product Closure** — project / policy-research body.
   Purpose: test whether product lifecycle systems remain practically usable at the point where a resident holds the actual object, including repair, reuse, return, recycling, recovery, and lawful disposal.
   Archive status: not adequately represented as a named project folder in `apo`; archived in this commit under `california-product-closure/`.

3. **Property Risk, Value & Options Map** — project / reusable research framework.

## B. Frameworks, models, and conceptual systems

22. **STUR + Closure** — Space, Time, Use, Responsibility, Closure. Cross-project framework; not itself a single project.
23. **Authority Map / Government Authority Framework** — government level → institution → authority → procedure → decision → review → record. A navigation aid, not a universal legal sequence.
24. **Twelve-committee / 4x3 / 6-pair model** — conceptual design metaphor for organizing authority and overlap. It remains a metaphor/research hypothesis unless independently grounded.
25. **Civic Flow evidence/feedback bridge** — REPORT → ROUTE → RECEIPT → STATUS → USE/REVIEW, with provenance and privacy classes.
26. **Closure principles** — ability to correct, transfer, complete, or end a relationship; distinguish routing, identification, collection, movement, and status change from actual resolution.
27. **Information provenance model** — preserve original input, transformation, human revision, and later transformation rather than collapsing them.
28. **Public access / library-access principle** — civic information should remain usable without requiring AI or specialized technology.
29. **Proportionality / reversible intervention principle** — use the smallest durable intervention capable of serving the public purpose while preserving future choice.
30. **Minimal functional continuity architecture** — shared building layer plus resident layer; measure loads before sizing.
31. **Information substrate / universal information-space concept** — separate persistent information from representations and presentation; still an unresolved technical research question.
32. **Information half-life model** — existence → accessibility → searchability → discoverability → preservation → independent recoverability.
33. **Civic Space Exchange** — concept within modular public infrastructure for matching partial/temporary property space with modular public-service needs.

## F. AI-assisted research / closure case

49. **AI-Assisted Closure Experience** — anonymized case study / research note, not a complaint and not an institutional evaluation.
    The experience involved using AI to develop a closure-oriented public-interest proposal and then contacting AI-related organizations. Responses exhibited three process patterns: an intake channel that had closed/redirected, a channel requiring authenticated continuation, and a request converted into a case pending review.
    The archive must not include personal email addresses, ticket IDs, account identifiers, unnecessary quotations, or re-identifying details.
    Status: newly archived in `ai-assisted-closure-experience/`.
    Significance: an example in which the communication channel itself can be examined using STUR + Closure.

## Relationship notes

The following are related but should not be silently merged:

- Civic Flow Chart and California Product Closure share the closure/practical-usability method but address different systems.
- Property Risk & Options Map and Seabright Plaza are framework/application, not two identical projects.
- Vertical Commons Housing and Santa Cruz Modular Public Infrastructure overlap around modularity but have different primary purposes.
- WAYFINDER, Community Access, and Traveling Neighborhood Resource Network are related information/service layers but remain distinct bodies of work.
- Daily Information System, Information Archaeology, and Internet Complexity share information-access concerns but study different problems.
- Minimal Functional Continuity is a resilience framework; its experimental energy-harvesting ideas are not automatically separate projects.
- Marine Research remains environmental research; it should not be merged into civic-flow or closure merely because process/navigation questions may apply.
- Temporary Heavy Construction and Modular Material Recovery are engineering/conceptual research directions and should remain separate.
- AI-assisted closure is a cross-cutting case study that informs both Civic Flow and Product Closure, but it is archived separately here so its provenance is not lost.
