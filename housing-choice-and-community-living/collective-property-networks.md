# Collective Property Networks

## Ten questions
1. What is the purpose of a shared property network?
2. Who would own or control participating properties?
3. How could people join, leave, or move between locations?
4. How would costs, repairs, and maintenance be funded?
5. How would insurance and liability be handled?
6. What rules would protect residents from exclusion or abuse?
7. How would decisions be made and disputes resolved?
8. How could accessibility and different needs be accommodated?
9. How would records, privacy, and personal data be protected?
10. Which legal structures should be compared first?
