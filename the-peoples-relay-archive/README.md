# The People's Relay — Archive Package

**Status:** concept / working archive / not a finished product

This folder preserves a developing body of ideas around **The People's Relay**, a privacy-preserving civic communications concept. It is intentionally an archive of possibilities rather than a declaration that the proposed system exists or that every hypothesis is established fact.

## Archive principle

> **Preserve the possibility before deciding what it is.**

The surrounding repository, `apo`, is treated as an **Archive of Possibilities**. This folder is self-contained so the material can be studied without requiring the original chat.

## Contents

- `MASTER_CLASS_COMMUNICATION.md` — working framework for a possible master class on communication.
- `RELAY_CONCEPT.md` — evolving concept for The People's Relay and its human-centered communications architecture.
- `DESIGN_GUARDRAILS.md` — explicit boundaries intended to prevent useful communications infrastructure from becoming an autonomous influence, retaliation, surveillance, or disruption system.
- `CONVERSATION_NOTES.md` — provenance and context notes preserving important observations, questions, metaphors, and unresolved ideas from the originating discussion.

## Epistemic status

The material mixes observations, design proposals, metaphors, philosophical reflections, hypotheses, and questions for future research. Where a claim would require historical, scientific, anthropological, legal, or technical verification, it is labeled as a question or hypothesis rather than presented as settled fact.

A recurring working rule is:

> **Dream weird. Verify everything.**

## Relationship to the project

The People's Relay is a possible project that emerged from this material. This archive does not establish an organization, nonprofit, deployed network, product, or operational communications service. It preserves the concept so those questions can be examined later.

## Core working idea

> **The purpose of the Relay is not to give people power over other people. It is to give people power over their own ability to communicate.**

A related human-authorization boundary:

> **The AI may write it. The Relay may carry it. Only you may send it.**
