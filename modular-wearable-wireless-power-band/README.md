# Modular Wearable Wireless Power Band

**Status:** PROPOSED / SPECULATIVE  
**Repository:** APO  
**Related project:** `emergency-ai-secretary-watch/`  
**Related file:** `emergency-ai-secretary-watch/modular-watch-band-and-wearable-mount-concept.md`

## Purpose

Explore a removable, modular wearable power band that can provide supplemental wireless energy to a smartwatch, sleep tracker, fitness tracker, or similar wearable without requiring the primary device to be removed from the wrist.

## Core Concept

The power system could take several forms:

- Flexible battery band.
- Multiple small connected battery pucks.
- Slap-wrap metal-band format.
- A removable band charged through USB-C, a wireless charging pad, or a dock.
- A solar-assisted exterior for supplemental trickle charging.

The watch remains worn while the compatible band supplies energy through a controlled wireless charging interface.

## Proposed Design Directions

1. **Wear-and-assist mode:** The band supplies energy while the watch remains on the wrist.
2. **Remove-and-charge mode:** The band is removed and charged independently.
3. **Modular puck mode:** Battery modules can be added, removed, relocated, or replaced.
4. **Slap-wrap mode:** A flexible metal-band structure allows rapid application and removal.
5. **Adjustable hygiene mode:** The band can slide or shift up and down the arm for cleaning, drying, and repositioning.
6. **Solar-assisted mode:** A flexible photovoltaic layer provides supplemental energy when light is available.

## Design Principle

> The battery is a removable power module; the band is one way of carrying and positioning it.

## Important Limits

This is a concept record, not a validated design. Battery safety, thermal limits, wireless alignment, charging efficiency, comfort, waterproofing, sensor interference, and compatibility require engineering research and testing.

Capacity should be evaluated in watt-hours (Wh), not only milliamp-hours (mAh), because voltage is required to understand stored energy.

## Evidence Categories

- **KNOWN:** Rechargeable batteries, wireless power transfer, USB-C charging, modular enclosures, and flexible photovoltaic materials exist in various forms.
- **CALCULATED:** Energy budget, weight, capacity, heat, wireless losses, and solar yield must be calculated.
- **TESTED:** No prototype testing has been documented in this folder yet.
- **PROPOSED:** A removable, modular wearable wireless power band.
- **SPECULATIVE:** A broadly compatible future standard for wearable power accessories.
- **UNKNOWN:** Practical comfort, efficiency, safety, and cross-device compatibility.
