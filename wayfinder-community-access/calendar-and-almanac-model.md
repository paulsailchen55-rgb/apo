# WAYFINDER Calendar and Community Almanac Model

## The problem

Different communities operate on different clocks:

- standardized civil calendar
- pay and benefit cycles
- school and academic calendars
- business/fiscal quarters
- agricultural cycles
- sports seasons
- civic/government schedules
- religious calendars
- local ecological cycles

WAYFINDER should not pretend one of these is the single “true” calendar.

## Two-layer model

### Human coordination clock

Use the standardized calendar for dates, interoperability, appointments, and publishing.

The first of the month can be a publication convention without being treated as a natural or sacred beginning.

### Living/community clock

Add local context such as:
- planting/sprouting
- flowering/fruiting
- harvest
- seasonal weather
- school terms
- sports seasons
- community events
- local work cycles

This can make the calendar more like an almanac than a simple event list.

## “ONE”

A possible symbolic monthly convention is to treat the first as **ONE** — one community — rather than claiming that it is cosmically or naturally the first day.

This is a naming experiment, not a settled requirement.

## Historical lineage

The project is conceptually influenced by Farmers’ Almanac-style practical seasonal reference and recurring digest publications. Exact historical publication details should be verified separately if they become part of a public historical account.

## Compatibility

Do not replace ordinary dates with a new chronology. Generate ordinary dates/ICS/calendar entries first, with ecological/community-cycle information as an additional layer.
