# Annual Citizenship / Housing Transition Commendation

This folder preserves the development of a personal commendation letter recognizing public servants, program workers, housing staff, community organizations, neighbors, and others whose work formed part of the network through which Paul Statchen moved from lifelong intermittent homelessness and housing instability into sustained housing.

The outward letter is intentionally simple. The supporting archive preserves the deeper personal, civic, spiritual, philosophical, housing, and research context.

Related research: Public Pathways from Homelessness to Housing: Santa Cruz City and County, 2020-Present.

Current status: prototype letter drafted; supporting research prompt drafted; detailed project record organized; recipient-specific personalization remains future work; exact personal dates are not required for the letter.

Central idea: I passed through a difficult civic and community network. People helped me. I reached sustained housing. I want to recognize those people and encourage the community to keep building pathways for others.
