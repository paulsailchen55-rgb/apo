# Unresolved Questions

These questions remain open for future research. They are written to invite investigation, comparison, and community participation rather than to assume a predetermined conclusion.

## Ten questions for further inquiry
1. What counts as meaningful choice under housing scarcity?
2. Which programs permit declining an offer without penalty?
3. How do local rules affect mobile, vehicle-based, or shared housing?
4. Which collective ownership structures are practical and lawful?
5. How can residents control records, privacy, and mobility?
6. What evidence distinguishes systemic patterns from individual experience?
7. How should housing options be funded without creating new forms of dependency?
8. What governance arrangements protect residents from exclusion or abuse?
9. How can accessibility and differing household needs be addressed?
10. What is the next specific research step, and who should be consulted?
