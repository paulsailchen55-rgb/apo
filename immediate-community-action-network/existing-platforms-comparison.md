# Existing Platforms and Comparative Review Plan

Related systems include JustServe, HelpMatch, Neighbor Powered, Kaleo, church-management systems, volunteer centers, and food-bank volunteer portals. They provide useful models for opportunity listings, organizational coordination, scheduling, and registration.

The proposed project differs by making immediate, low-barrier action the primary search: what can a person do in the next 15 minutes, hour, or evening? Existing systems should be studied rather than dismissed. The review should record account requirements, onboarding steps, screening, accessibility, location data, APIs or feeds, public pages, update practices, and whether no-account browsing or participation is possible.

The first prototype should compare a small number of real listings in one locality before attempting nationwide coverage.
