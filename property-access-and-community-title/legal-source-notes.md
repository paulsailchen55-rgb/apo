# Legal Source Notes

## California Department of Real Estate

The California Department of Real Estate Reference Book, Chapter 5, "Title to Real Property," is a relevant starting point.

Source: https://www.dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/refbook/ref05.pdf

The chapter states that other lawfully created entities may hold title to real property or interests in real property and specifically discusses corporations, trusts, LLCs, and LLPs. It distinguishes different corporation categories and discusses practical legal, tax, estate-planning, and financing consequences of entity and trust ownership.

The current California DRE also publishes a 2026 Real Estate Law volume containing statutes and regulations relevant to real estate practice:

https://dre.ca.gov/Publications/RealEstateLaw.html

## Evidence discipline

Do not use a textbook's description of a legal category as proof that the category remains unchanged in current law. Current statutes, regulations, case law, and authoritative agency materials should control the legal analysis.

Do not infer that because an entity can hold title, it solves the problem of initial capital, financing, insurance, governance, taxes, maintenance, or individual access.
