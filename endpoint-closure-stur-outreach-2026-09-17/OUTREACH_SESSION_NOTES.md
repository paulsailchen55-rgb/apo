# Outreach Session Notes

## Context

This package captures the outreach strategy developed while extending the endpoint-letter campaign into additional communities.

A recurring operational problem was repeated reuse of the same organizations or multiple contacts within one organization. The working correction was to maintain a recipient ledger and favor breadth across distinct organizations.

## Environmental layer

The environmental layer connects the endpoint principle to physical products, waste, recycling, stewardship, circularity, and environmental access. The conceptual bridge is:

- identification is not disposition;
- collection is not closure;
- movement is not closure;
- a service existing somewhere is not the same as a practically usable pathway;
- responsibility should include an actual endpoint for the object.

## Builders / technologists layer

The technology layer is intentionally broader than policy advocacy. It includes:

- civic technologists;
- open-source communities;
- hackerspaces;
- makerspaces;
- software builders;
- privacy/security technologists;
- accessibility and human-computer-interaction communities;
- hardware and repair communities.

The central systems-design question is: if a system provides a way to enter, can it also provide a clear and usable way to leave?

## Loose-end pass

After the principal categories, the conversation proposed a further pass through organizations that had not yet been contacted, including additional civil-activist and news organizations. Repeats were permitted only as a fallback concept; the preferred operational rule remained one organization per slot and no unnecessary duplicate outreach.

## Verification rule

Some addresses surfaced during exploratory searching were uncertain, obsolete, role-specific, or not appropriate for the intended outreach. They must not be treated as verified merely because they appeared in a search result or conversation draft.

The campaign's recipient status should distinguish:

- verified/current contact;
- plausible but unresolved contact;
- inappropriate role-specific contact;
- previously contacted organization;
- organization intentionally skipped.

The existing `santa-cruz-endpoint-letter-outreach-archive/MASTER_RECIPIENT_LIST.csv` is the campaign's primary recipient ledger. This folder does not replace it.

## Scope

The endpoint letter is a policy proposal and framing device. It is not presented here as established law, a proven empirical conclusion, or an endorsement by any recipient organization.

## Preservation note

This archive preserves the possibility of the idea and the reasoning around it. It does not require deciding now whether the proposal will become legislation, regulation, software, a design standard, a research project, or remain a correspondence experiment.
