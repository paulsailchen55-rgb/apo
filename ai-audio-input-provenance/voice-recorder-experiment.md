# Voice Recorder Experiment

## Device and workflow
- Philips VoiceTracer with internal storage and possible SD-card support.
- Spoken notes recorded in natural sections, often approximately 15–20 minutes.
- One experiment played recorded speech through the VoiceTracer speaker.
- ChatGPT's microphone captured the speaker playback.
- A possible alternative is direct file transfer through a USB-C cable.

## Comparison points
- Transcription accuracy.
- Recognition of playback versus live speech.
- Background noise and speaker distortion.
- Ease of transferring files.
- Preservation of original recordings.
- Battery and storage demands.

## Practical safeguards
Keep original recordings until transfer and processing have been confirmed. Do not treat an automatic live/playback classification as certain without adequate evidence.
