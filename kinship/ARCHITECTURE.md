# KINSHIP Architecture

This document will become the human-readable architectural overview.

Current conceptual architecture:

- repetitive dome structure
- buoyant lower pod
- four articulated load-bearing/environmental arms
- adaptable enclosure and protective skins
- anchoring and controlled release
- terrestrial and aquatic modes
- potential interconnection between habitats
- optional sensing, autonomy, robotics, and higher intelligence

Detailed engineering claims remain subject to the evidence-status system.
