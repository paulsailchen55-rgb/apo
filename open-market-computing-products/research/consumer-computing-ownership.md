# Consumer Computing Ownership

## Research question

What does ownership mean when a product is both a physical object and a controlled computer system?

## Questions

- What rights should accompany ownership?
- Does buying hardware imply a right to modify its software?
- How should licenses affect ownership?
- What happens when software updates change functionality?
- What rights should survive manufacturer support ending?
- How does digital control alter traditional concepts of personal property?
