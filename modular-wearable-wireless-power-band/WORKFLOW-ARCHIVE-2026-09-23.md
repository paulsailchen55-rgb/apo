## Conversation Topic

Modular wearable wireless power band for continuous wearable use.

## User's Concept

The user proposed a battery band that could charge a watch wirelessly while the watch remains worn. The band could be flexible, composed of connected pucks, or use a slap-wrap metal-band format inspired by older slap bracelets. It could be charged through USB-C or wirelessly after removal. The user also proposed a solar exterior for ambient-light trickle charging and an adjustable band that can move up and down the arm for cleaning and repositioning.

The concept was connected to travelers, Fitbit and sleep-tracker users, and a possible future compatibility standard.
