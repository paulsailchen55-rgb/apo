# Conversation Inventory

## Primary Project

**California Adaptive Dome & Yurt Housing Pathways** — a county-by-county, California-first guidance and archival system for people and organizations that have already chosen dome- or yurt-based housing.

Classification: Project / potential software and documentation system.

## Supporting Bodies of Work

### 1. Regulatory Navigation Workflow
Classification: Framework / tool concept.

A flowchart and eventual AI/HTML interface that guides users through jurisdiction, zoning, dwelling classification, demolition or replacement, ADU possibilities, site constraints, permits, inspections, and professional review.

### 2. Community-Scale Dome and Yurt Development
Classification: Potential project / development pathway.

Includes connected structures, small dome or yurt communities, nonprofit development, cooperative arrangements, and alternatives to conventional large apartment forms.

### 3. Historical and Cultural Context
Classification: Research direction / supporting reference.

Investigate Indigenous architectural traditions and the relationship between housing, landscape, materials, environmental adaptation, and cultural meaning. Avoid unsupported generalizations.

### 4. Archaeological Hypotheses
Classification: Speculative research questions.

Includes hypotheses concerning circular depressions, mounds, collapsed structures, and later excavation or reuse. These must remain clearly labeled as speculative unless independently documented.

### 5. Case Studies
Classification: Research direction.

Potential case studies include an unidentified Los Angeles dome community that encountered regulatory or operational consequences and a plastic dome structure observed in the Boulder Creek area. Exact identities and histories remain unresolved.

### 6. Accessibility and Inclusion
Classification: Design requirement.

The system should support people of different ages, abilities, technical backgrounds, and resources through plain-language explanations, printable workflows, phone-compatible access, and human contact pathways.

## Explicit Non-Goals

- The project is not intended to persuade people to choose domes or yurts.
- It is not a substitute for official approval, licensed professional judgment, or current jurisdiction-specific verification.
- It should not silently merge with the separate KINSHIP habitat project.
