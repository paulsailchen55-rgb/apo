# KINSHIP Roadmap

The roadmap will evolve as requirements and evidence improve.

## Conceptual prototype sequence

P0 — geometry model  
P1 — structural dome  
P2 — enclosure prototype  
P3 — buoyant pod  
P4 — four-arm testbed  
P5 — integrated land prototype  
P6 — floating prototype  
P7 — aquatic mobility prototype  
P8 — environmental prototype  
P9 — occupied prototype  
P10 — certification/deployment prototype

No prototype stage should be interpreted as validated until its stated test criteria are defined and met.
