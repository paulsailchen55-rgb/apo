# Two-Minute Speech Framework

The two-minute speech is the universal preparation format. Actual limits must be determined from current official rules.

## Suggested structure

1. Greeting and identity
2. Problem or opportunity
3. Proposed idea
4. Evidence and public benefit
5. Specific request
6. Optional participation or follow-up pathway

Generate shorter or longer variants when appropriate. Preserve the user's meaning, distinguish evidence from proposals, and avoid unsupported certainty.

## Timing

Support approximate 30-second, 2-minute, 5-minute, and meeting-length versions. Timing should be estimated from the actual script and confirmed by the user through practice.
