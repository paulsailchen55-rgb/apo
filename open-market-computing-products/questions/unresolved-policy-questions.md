# Unresolved Policy Questions

1. What legally and technically qualifies as an open-market computing product?
2. Is open source necessary, or is meaningful owner control sufficient?
3. Which hardware interfaces must be documented?
4. Must the bootloader be unlockable?
5. What firmware components can legitimately remain proprietary?
6. How should security-sensitive components be treated?
7. How should safety-critical systems be treated?
8. How should regulated radios and communications systems be treated?
9. Could governments encourage open products without mandating them?
10. Would procurement policy be more practical than a universal requirement?
11. Should different computing categories have different openness criteria?
12. Could an open product requirement unintentionally reduce competition or innovation?
13. What existing laws already provide part of the desired result?
14. What evidence exists regarding education and technical literacy?
15. How can independent developers certify or distribute software safely?
16. How can a manufacturer retain a sustainable business around open hardware?
17. Could an open product category become a bridge between consumer electronics and educational computing?
18. How should international differences in law and regulation be handled?

Open research questions.
