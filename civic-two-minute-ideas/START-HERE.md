# Start Here

1. Select one idea from an APO project.
2. Identify the jurisdiction and intended audience.
3. Retrieve current official rules, meeting information, agenda, and relevant project documents.
4. Record source URLs, dates, versions, and unresolved conflicts.
5. Separate facts, observations, calculations, proposals, and unknowns.
6. Match the idea to general comment, a scheduled agenda item, a hearing, or a meeting request.
7. Select participation mode: phone, video/Zoom, in-person, written, or private appointment.
8. Generate a mode-appropriate core statement, normally targeting two minutes.
9. Optionally explore an artistic lab concept, subject to rules and practical constraints.
10. Review, revise, and approve before use.
