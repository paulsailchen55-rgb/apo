# Seabright Plaza — First-Pass Research Context

This file complements community-question-routing.md.

The reviewed City project page identified two SB330-only preliminary applications for 1214 Soquel Avenue. The City described preliminary concepts for a six-story senior living project and a six-story 100%-affordable housing project, both with 5,000 square feet of commercial space and demolition of existing buildings.

Community concerns remain questions until verified:
- What existing businesses and community functions would be affected?
- What is required by law versus proposed by the applicant?
- What physical-condition evidence supports demolition?
- What transportation, parking, loading, utility, drainage, fire/life-safety, environmental, tree, and accessibility studies exist?
- What protections or relocation arrangements exist for current businesses?
- What is the actual SB330 completeness/status and procedural clock?
- Which authority ultimately reviews and decides each entitlement?

This file records the questions and first-pass status; it does not answer them.
