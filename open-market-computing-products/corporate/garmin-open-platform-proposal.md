# Garmin Open Platform Proposal

## Purpose

Develop a future proposal to Garmin asking whether it would create a deliberately open product within its otherwise proprietary product ecosystem.

## The proposal

Garmin would continue making and selling its normal integrated products exactly as it chooses.

In addition, Garmin could create a product or product edition intended for people who want to develop on the underlying computing hardware.

A particularly useful model would be an open/developer edition based on an existing Garmin watch platform, using substantially the same physical hardware rather than creating a deliberately simplified "maker" device.

## What is being requested

The request is not:

- open all Garmin firmware;
- open all Garmin products;
- release all Garmin intellectual property;
- abandon proprietary algorithms or services;
- give up patents or industrial designs;
- make Garmin's entire company open source.

The request is:

> Would Garmin consider selling one officially supported open hardware/software product, or a small open product line, that gives owners and independent developers meaningful legal and technical ability to modify and develop the device?

## Possible architecture

The eventual technical proposal should investigate:

- documented hardware interfaces;
- sensor access;
- display access;
- storage access;
- power-management interfaces;
- GPS and other positioning interfaces;
- Bluetooth interfaces;
- cellular/satellite interfaces where legally and technically possible;
- microphone and speaker interfaces;
- bootloader/developer access;
- firmware replacement or modification;
- driver documentation;
- software development tools;
- an open reference implementation;
- clear boundaries around proprietary Garmin services and technology.

The actual minimum requirements should be determined through technical research rather than assumed in advance.

## Why this could be a market

The proposed product could serve customers who are not looking for another finished consumer appliance.

Potential users include:

- independent developers;
- researchers;
- universities;
- students;
- accessibility developers;
- environmental researchers;
- artists;
- makers;
- small companies;
- startups;
- experimental-interface designers;
- people who want to understand and modify their own computing devices.

The open product could also create applications and communities that Garmin itself would not have resources or reasons to build internally.

## Coexistence with normal Garmin products

The proposal is explicitly a parallel-market model.

A customer who wants a finished Garmin experience can continue buying the ordinary Garmin product.

A customer who wants a development platform can buy the open edition.

The existence of the open edition does not require Garmin to convert its entire product line.

## Central question

> You already make excellent closed, integrated products. Would you consider creating one officially supported open hardware/software product, using the quality and hardware engineering of an existing commercial platform, for people who want to develop and experiment with what the machine can become?

## Status

Potential corporate proposal. Do not send until the technical architecture, legal/IP boundary, and current Garmin developer capabilities have been researched.
