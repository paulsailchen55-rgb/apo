# Minimal Functional Continuity — Master Idea Log

Purpose: preserve the broader reasoning behind the Minimal Functional Continuity concept so ideas are not lost when the short external packet changes.

Core threads: minimal continuity between normal service and total loss; continuity as objective and technology as means; distributed shared plus individual layers; minimum rather than extreme normalcy; intermittent operation; actual load measurement; efficiency before capacity; redundant storage; solar and supplemental wind; earth battery as measured low-power experiment; future energy harvesting; technology-neutral safety evaluation; hidden-risk avoidance; lifecycle and regional-service questions; subsidy and technology-choice questions; resilience independent of wealth; lease/property constraints; future residential design; food, water, cooking, communications and lighting; pilot before mandate; authority routing; preservation of future choice.

Demonstration context: a roughly 16-person residential building was used as a planning example. Shared resources could support common functions while individual self-contained units could support resident-specific loads.

Measurement context: an AirDoctor nameplate was recorded at 95 W. A basic box fan, Chromebook, phone and router were also considered. Exact draw should be measured rather than inferred from generic ranges.

Important distinctions: this archive contains observations, planning assumptions, hypotheses, design proposals and questions. It is not engineering approval, legal advice or a universal performance claim.

Current status: the concept was sent to Santa Cruz City and County contacts in September 2026. Future government-facing documents should default to accessible PDF when no receiving agency specifies another format; agency-specific requirements override this convention.
