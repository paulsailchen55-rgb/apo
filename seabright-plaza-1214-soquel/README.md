# 1214 Soquel Avenue / Seabright Plaza

Archive of site-specific research, questions, provenance and outreach context from September 2026.

This is not a legal conclusion or project approval. Community concerns are preserved as questions until supported by current records.

## Working method
Preserve the source of each concern. Separate observation, reported experience, documented fact, interpretation, hypothesis and proposal. Route each question to the person or institution with the relevant authority.

## Research topics
Zoning and development status; building scale; traffic and parking; environmental review; trees and stormwater; fire and life safety; utilities and loading; existing businesses and community assets; and possible alternatives.

## Broader question
Explore whether housing and development needs can be addressed while preserving lawful process, safety, existing community assets and future options.

> Preserve the possibility before deciding what it is.
