# Provenance and Conversation History

Each addition should identify its source: current conversation, separate chat, voice recording, field note, lived experience, external research, or later interpretation.

When practical, record the approximate date, source, type of material, related documents, and whether the item is new, revised, or repeated.

Material from a separate conversation should be identified before incorporation. Later developments should not silently rewrite the original record. Observations, hypotheses, and verified claims must remain distinguishable.
