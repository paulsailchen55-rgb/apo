# Agenda Analysis

The system should retrieve the current official agenda and identify where an idea may properly fit.

## Steps

1. Find the official agenda and meeting packet.
2. Identify general public comment, scheduled items, hearings, consent items, and regular business.
3. Match the idea to relevant agenda language without overstating relevance.
4. Check whether comments must be limited to agenda items or may address broader jurisdictional matters.
5. Determine the published speaking-order and registration process.
6. Explain whether the user has any legitimate choice about timing or order.
7. Produce a source-linked recommendation and mark uncertainties.

The system must never encourage disruption, false registration, misrepresentation, or interference with other speakers.
