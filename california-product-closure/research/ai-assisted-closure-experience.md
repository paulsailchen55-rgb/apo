# AI-Assisted Closure Experience — Anonymized Research Note

## Classification
Research note / case study supporting the California Product Closure project.

## Provenance and privacy

This note records a real process experience in anonymized form. It is not a complaint about a particular organization or person and is not an evaluation of institutional quality, legality, competence, motive, or outcome.

Personal names, private email addresses, account identifiers, case/ticket numbers, and unnecessary quotations are intentionally omitted. Anonymization cannot guarantee privacy.

## Observed process pattern

A public-interest closure proposal was developed with AI assistance and then sent to several AI-related organizations. The resulting responses illustrated three different channel states:

1. An intake channel had closed or redirected responsibility.
2. A communication channel required continuation through an authenticated account/support workflow.
3. A request had been converted into a case pending staff review.

These are observations about process behavior. They are not by themselves evidence of a closure failure.

## Lifecycle model

**PERSON → CONTACT → CLASSIFICATION → ROUTING → AUTHENTICATION / CASE → REVIEW → RESOLUTION / REDIRECTION / CLOSURE**

Questions for each stage:
- Where does the relationship/request exist?
- What is the channel for?
- What information is carried forward?
- What clock governs the next state?
- Who receives the request, and who has actual decision responsibility?
- What happens if the person does nothing?
- Can the person correct, withdraw, transfer, complete, or end the request?
- What remains after completion?

## Closure distinctions

- Identification ≠ disposition.
- Collection ≠ closure.
- Routing ≠ resolution.
- Digital access ≠ practical access.
- A status change is not necessarily completion of the underlying relationship.

## AI-specific observation

AI did not create email routing, ticketing, authentication, departmental boundaries, or review queues. AI may nevertheless participate in classification, routing, summarization, storage, recommendation, or action, and may increase speed, scale, persistence, or automation.

The research question is therefore:

> When an AI-assisted system carries a person's request, record, obligation, or conflict forward, how does the system recognize that the underlying relationship has been resolved, redirected, expired, corrected, or legitimately ended?

## Evidence discipline

**EXPERIENCE → OBSERVED RESPONSE → LIFECYCLE MODEL → DESIGN QUESTION → FUTURE TEST**

The case should not be used to infer institutional intent or to condemn any participant.

## Relationship to Product Closure

The analogy is:

**PRODUCT → OWNER/USER → USE → REPAIR/RETURN/RECOVERY → ENDPOINT → RECORD**

versus:

**REQUEST/RECORD → USER → USE → TRANSFER/CORRECTION/REVIEW → RESOLUTION → RETENTION/DELETION/ARCHIVE**

The common question is whether the final state is merely declared by the system or is practically understandable and usable by the person who must act.

## Status

Anonymized working research note. Further work requires verified external evidence before making claims about any organization or generalizing from this case.
