# Initial Note — September 19, 2026

This project was identified during a dual experiment:

1. Testing methods for moving spoken notes from a Philips VoiceTracer to ChatGPT, including speaker playback into the ChatGPT microphone and direct file transfer.
2. Exploring housing policy and meaningful housing choice for people experiencing homelessness.

The audio-input project should be maintained independently from the housing research while retaining the housing discussion as context for why reliable note capture and organization matter.
