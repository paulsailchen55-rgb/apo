# Lived Experience and Field Notes

This document preserves direct observations separately from externally verified claims.

Themes include a long experience of homelessness, the difference between receiving one housing offer and being presented with meaningful alternatives, and uncertainty about whether a recent lease felt fully official or secure.

The archive should distinguish:

- Direct personal experience
- Interpretation of that experience
- Broader social or economic claims
- Questions requiring legal, policy, or empirical research

The purpose is preservation and inquiry, not the assumption that one experience automatically proves a universal condition.
