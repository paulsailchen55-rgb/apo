# Step 1 — Core Purpose and Context

## Core purpose
Develop a practical, repairable, and adaptable mobility and property-support system that helps people experiencing homelessness or unstable housing transport, protect, and retain their belongings while maintaining access to transportation, healthcare, employment, legal services, and housing opportunities.

## Key contexts
These are design contexts, not complaints:

- Encampment sweeps and forced relocation
- Belongings being discarded, confiscated, damaged, or inadequately stored
- Theft, coercion, or exploitation by other individuals
- Pressure to sell or trade essential belongings
- Orders to move that may lead to citations, arrest, or other legal consequences
- Loss of identification, medication, phones, tools, documents, clothing, and sentimental possessions
- Separation from belongings during appointments, work, or service access
- Repeated displacement disrupting healthcare and support relationships
- Difficulty transporting possessions through a city or onto public transit
- Difficulty recovering property after removal, confiscation, theft, or abandonment
- Loss of mobility equipment and other disability-related necessities

These contexts require later jurisdiction-specific documentation and should not automatically be treated as identical events or settled legal conclusions.
