# Virtual Twin — User Authority, Provenance, and Epistemic Control

**Status:** PROPOSED

## Core principle

> **The person remains the authority over their own Twin.**

A Virtual Twin is intended to represent a person, so the person should be able to understand, inspect, correct, control, and override what the Twin knows, infers, does, and shares.

## Why this matters

A normal AI can give a person a wrong answer. A Virtual Twin introduces a different problem: it can potentially develop a **wrong understanding of the person it represents**.

That makes it important to preserve a visible distinction between:

- **Profile information** — facts the person explicitly publishes.
- **User-provided information** — things the person tells the Twin in conversation or other interactions.
- **AI-generated or inferred interpretation** — patterns, conclusions, assumptions, or other material generated by the system.

These categories should not silently become indistinguishable.

## User controls

A trustworthy Virtual Twin should eventually provide clear ways for the person to:

- inspect what information the Twin is using
- distinguish explicit information from AI inference
- correct inaccurate information
- export information
- remove information
- have private or temporary conversations that do not contribute to the Twin
- understand what happens to derived information when a conversation is deleted
- reset what the Twin has learned without deleting the entire profile
- control what the Twin does and shares on the person's behalf

## Accessibility connection

This concept emerged alongside feedback about Virtual Twin accessibility.

Built-in voice input and read-aloud can make the interaction more accessible, particularly when browser or device accessibility tools are unavailable, difficult to configure, or cannot persist on shared devices.

Accessibility and privacy therefore intersect: the person should be able to **communicate with the Twin and control the Twin** through the interface itself.

## Design question

> **How can a Virtual Twin represent a person without gradually becoming an authority about that person?**

## Deeper question

> **What does it mean for a machine to represent a person while the person remains the authority over the representation?**
