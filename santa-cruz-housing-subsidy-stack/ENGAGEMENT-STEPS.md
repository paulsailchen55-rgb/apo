# Engagement Steps

This is a research-to-civic-communication workflow, not a predetermined policy position.

1. Complete the evidence register.
2. Verify current Santa Cruz County and California requirements.
3. Identify the specific interface being discussed.
4. State the measurable mechanism: cost, delay, capacity, participation, lease-up, retention, or risk.
5. Distinguish documented effect from hypothesis.
6. Identify affected parties: household, owner/provider, developer, agency, infrastructure provider, community.
7. Identify possible tradeoffs.
8. Ask for data or a pilot where evidence is insufficient.
9. Preserve the question and response in the archive.
10. Update the research before making a new civic claim.
