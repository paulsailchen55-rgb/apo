# 1.6 — AI, Archiving, and Recovering Languages

## Project Proposal

If movement can be translated into music, and music can be treated as a structured language, AI may help archive relationships among ancient movement practices, martial arts, sound systems, ritual patterns, and historical languages.

The goal would not be to assume that a lost language can be recovered automatically or perfectly. The goal would be to assemble evidence, compare patterns, identify uncertainty, and generate hypotheses for further human review.

## Possible Evidence Sources

- Historical descriptions of martial arts and movement practices.
- Surviving demonstrations, images, diagrams, and recordings.
- Linguistic records and translations.
- Musical structures and rhythmic traditions.
- Oral histories and community-held knowledge.
- Archaeological, anthropological, and historical research.
- Contemporary practitioners who can provide contextual interpretation.

## Proposed AI Workflow

1. Collect source material with provenance.
2. Separate direct evidence from interpretation and speculation.
3. Extract movement features such as posture, timing, rhythm, direction, balance, pause, and interaction.
4. Translate features into structured descriptions or music.
5. Compare patterns across sources while preserving cultural distinctions.
6. Generate possible interpretations with confidence levels and explicit uncertainty.
7. Submit results for review by relevant scholars, practitioners, language communities, and cultural custodians.

## Safeguards

- Do not present generated reconstructions as authentic historical recovery.
- Do not erase cultural ownership or context.
- Do not infer a person's mental state or diagnosis from movement alone.
- Preserve multiple interpretations where evidence is incomplete.
- Record what is measured, inferred, imagined, and generated.

## Central Question

Can AI help us recover relationships among movement, music, language, and worldview while remaining honest about what the historical record can and cannot establish?
