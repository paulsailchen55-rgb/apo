# Residential Continuity Framework

## Concept

Resilience can be treated as an ordinary residential design requirement rather than only an emergency program.

A building can have:
- individual continuity capacity
- shared building continuity
- emergency information access
- common charging
- low-energy cooking options
- lighting
- cooling/air movement
- communications
- water/food continuity where appropriate

## Design question

What can be made standard, repairable, and understandable before a crisis occurs?

## Relationship

This framework connects Minimal Functional Continuity with residential design, lease infrastructure, and modular public infrastructure, but remains distinct from the Vertical Commons proposal.
