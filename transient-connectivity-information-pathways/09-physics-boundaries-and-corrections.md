# 09 — Physics Boundaries, Corrections, and Hard-to-Understand Issues

This project crosses everyday observation, fluid dynamics, information theory, astrophysics, philosophy, and theology. Analogy must not be mistaken for evidence.

## Sink vortex ≠ wormhole

A water vortex is a fluid-dynamical structure. A wormhole is a theoretical spacetime geometry. They may share abstract language about connectivity without being physically equivalent.

## Vortex ≠ communication channel

A vortex can participate in physical flow, but a communication channel requires a definable signal, encoding, noise environment, and receiver.

## Observation and quantum mechanics

Quantum measurement is not evidence that ordinary human attention can arbitrarily alter physical reality. Measurement is an interaction described by precise theory.

## Prayer and quantum mechanics

A theological claim about divine observation or transformation should not be presented as a consequence of the quantum measurement problem.

## Universe as observer

"The universe knowing itself" can be a philosophical description of self-modeling systems. It is not automatically evidence of divine intelligence.

## Humans as wormholes

The human "breath" or temporary-connection idea is a philosophical model of temporality and participation, not a claim that human bodies form astrophysical tunnels.

## Darkened realms

Hell and purgatory belong to theological traditions and interpretations. The mathematical model can represent degradation, separation, disorder, or destructive feedback without asserting a supernatural physical geography.

## Strongest shared abstraction

The most defensible interdisciplinary core is:

COUPLED SYSTEMS + BOUNDARIES + TRANSIENT PATHWAYS + STATE TRANSFORMATION + INFORMATION TRANSFER

That is where formal research should begin.
