## Boundaries

Resilience items belong to one family but remain distinct by function. Civic process/agency projects share provenance principles but are not one project. Information-access projects are related but technically different. Santa Cruz space/housing work spans general frameworks, public infrastructure, housing design and a site-specific case; these remain separate. Environmental research and the government-authority metaphor remain separate from active proposals.

## Archive rule

Do not silently merge unrelated work. Preserve user ideas, observations, interpretations, hypotheses, proposals, documented facts, external evidence and assistant-generated organization as different categories where the distinction matters.
