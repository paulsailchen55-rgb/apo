# Step 6 — Minimal First Prototype

## Prototype objective

Build and test a simple push-and-ride cargo trike that can safely transport belongings before attempting a complete shelter, storage, or digital-coordination system.

## Minimum components

- Stable three-wheel frame with a practical center of gravity.
- Comfortable push handle and controllable steering.
- Cargo platform or enclosed cargo area with basic tie-down points.
- Temporary fold-out seat and usable pedal/ride arrangement.
- Reliable brakes, parking stability, reflectors, and visibility features.
- Replaceable wheels and serviceable fasteners.
- Mounting provisions for future electric assistance, without requiring a motor in version one.

## Initial tests

1. Static load and balance testing.
2. Braking on level and sloped surfaces.
3. Push effort, turning radius, curb transitions, and rough-surface handling.
4. Seat deployment, entry/exit, and rider stability.
5. Cargo restraint, weather protection, and basic security.
6. Repair time, tool requirements, and component replacement.

## Explicit exclusions from version one

- Full sleeping platform, tent, or weather shelter.
- Integrated cellular tracking or complex software.
- Large-scale storage-pod network.
- Assumptions about transit acceptance or public-agency cooperation.

Prototype testing should be supervised, documented, and revised before real-world lending.
