# Contributing to KINSHIP

Contributions should preserve the distinction between evidence and hypothesis.

When adding material:

1. State what is being proposed or observed.
2. Identify evidence status.
3. Give sources, calculations, test data, or assumptions where available.
4. Do not convert conversation speculation into established fact.
5. Preserve unresolved questions instead of silently resolving them.
6. Prefer interoperable interfaces and locally adaptable implementation.
7. Record significant design changes in CHANGELOG.md.

A future formal contribution process may add coding, CAD, testing, review, and certification requirements.
