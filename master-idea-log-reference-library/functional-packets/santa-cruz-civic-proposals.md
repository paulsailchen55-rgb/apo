# Santa Cruz — Civic Proposals for Adaptable Development

Status: city-level proposal set; not adopted policy.

Purpose: identify city actions that could make future adaptation, repair, material stewardship, and procedural transparency easier without assuming a particular development outcome.

Possible city actions:
1. Establish a pilot for long-life infrastructure with changeable components.
2. Study how preservation of useful existing buildings can coexist with additional housing capacity.
3. Develop a voluntary or pilot building-passport/documentation standard.
4. Study material lifecycle and end-of-life documentation for major public or pilot projects.
5. Create a public demonstration of adaptable/modular construction.
6. Identify which current city requirements support or impede adaptation, repair, reuse, modularity, and future change.
7. Develop a civic process map showing the path from application through review, decision, construction, and future modification.
8. Make project records accessible through both digital channels and public-library/community access points.
9. Test a one-page choice/authority guide for complex development processes.
10. Evaluate distributed housing or multi-site approaches as one option among lawful housing strategies.

Each item should proceed through evidence, legal review, technical review, cost review, and a defined pilot or study rather than being treated as an established solution.

Central question: Can Santa Cruz meet current needs while preserving future choices?
