# WAYFINDER Santa Cruz Pilot

## Immediate scope

Test WAYFINDER in a defined walkable regional area around Santa Cruz rather than trying to enumerate every city.

The geographic boundary is intentionally unresolved. It should reflect practical access to useful destinations rather than municipal boundaries alone.

## Initial ten categories

1. Free Coffee & Tea
2. Free Food / Community Meals
3. Meditation & Mindfulness
4. Faith & Spirituality
5. Recovery & Peer Support
6. Volunteering / Community Cleanup
7. Library & Learning
8. Arts, Music & Creative Community
9. Activism & Civic
10. General Daytime Community

## Required fields

- activity name
- category
- date
- start/end time
- address
- city
- free/access status
- source URL
- contact if publicly available
- last verified
- verification status
- notes

## Pilot question

Can a useful current calendar be maintained with the AI and free/low-cost services already available?

A successful answer does not require every possible event. It requires enough accurate information to be practically useful.

## Regional logic

Do not assume every small town has ten categories or even one event in each category. A town may have only a few resources and may depend on a neighboring town.

The system should therefore support:
- no results
- nearby results
- recurring resources
- regional resources
- human-supplied information
- source accessibility limitations

## Scaling question

The number of U.S. cities/towns is not the main workload metric. The relevant metric is the number of **active, maintainable local information records** and the cost of verifying them.

Worldwide scaling should be treated as a later research question.
