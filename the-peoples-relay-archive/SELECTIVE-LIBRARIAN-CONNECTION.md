# Selective Librarian Connection

The People's Relay is concerned with individual control over communication.

The Selective Librarian adds a related information-access boundary: a system may assist a person in finding and organizing information, but the system should not quietly become the authority that decides what the person is permitted to know.

Possible flow:

OPEN RECORDS
→ RETRIEVAL / NAVIGATION
→ HUMAN INSPECTION
→ HUMAN DECISION
→ HUMAN AUTHORIZATION

The Relay's human-authorization principle therefore has an information analogue:

> AI may help find the evidence. The person decides what to do with it.

A Selective Librarian should preserve original sources, show provenance, expose retrieval reasons, permit broader searching, and avoid covert personalization or manipulation.
