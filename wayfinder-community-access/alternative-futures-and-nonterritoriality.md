# WAYFINDER Alternative Futures and Non-Territoriality

## Why this exists

A public symbol and information network can be useful, but it can also be copied, commercialized, weaponized, or turned into territorial/reputational signaling.

The project should therefore consider failure modes before deployment.

## Positive / solarpunk future

People use the symbol to find:
- free coffee
- meals
- libraries
- volunteering
- learning
- creative groups
- civic meetings
- community gatherings

People verify information and leave useful information for the next person.

## Failure futures

### Graffiti battle
Competing marks become a visual conflict.

### Territory machine
Groups treat symbols as claims of ownership over streets or neighborhoods.

### Reputation machine
Verification becomes a public scorecard against organizations.

### Corporate capture
The system becomes a ranked advertising product.

### Information decay
Outdated listings remain visible because nobody maintains them.

### AI distortion
The model turns uncertain or partial sources into confident claims.

## Design responses

- Standardized symbol as interface, not secret code.
- No territorial meaning.
- No public organization scores.
- Preserve source and verification status.
- Prefer status changes to permanent negative labels.
- Keep AI subordinate to structured source records.
- Make the system replaceable and portable.
- Keep public information useful without exposing unnecessary private data.

## Core principle

> Build something useful enough to spread, and carefully enough that it remains usable if people misuse it.

A healthy information system requires both openness and protection.

> **Keep the gate open enough for community to grow. Keep the garden strong enough to survive.**
