# AI-Assisted Closure Experience

## Classification
Cross-cutting research case study.

## Purpose

This folder preserves an anonymized experience in which AI was used to develop a public-interest proposal about closure, and the communication process with AI-related organizations became a real-world test case for the same closure questions.

It is deliberately separate from the Civic Flow Chart and California Product Closure projects so the provenance of the experience is preserved without forcing it into either project.

## Central loop

**HUMAN OBSERVATION → AI-ASSISTED MODEL → REAL-WORLD ACTION → OBSERVED RESPONSE → MODEL REVISION**

AI output is not automatically external evidence. External claims require independent verification.

## Why it matters

The experience demonstrates that closure questions can apply to a communication channel itself:
- a channel may be closed and redirected;
- a channel may require authenticated continuation;
- a request may become a case awaiting review.

A received, routed, or classified request is not necessarily a resolved request.

## STUR + Closure

**SPACE** — email, account, portal, support, safety, or other channel.

**TIME** — intake periods, response clocks, review periods, and expiration.

**USE** — the purpose and permitted category of each channel.

**RESPONSIBILITY** — receiving office versus actual decision authority.

**CLOSURE** — closed, redirected, pending, resolved, withdrawn, corrected, or otherwise ended.

## Non-condemnation rule

The public archive should omit:
- personal names where unnecessary;
- private contact information;
- ticket or case identifiers;
- account identifiers;
- unnecessary verbatim correspondence;
- details that make an anonymized participant readily identifiable.

Anonymization is a risk-reduction measure, not a guarantee of privacy.

## Design implications

A future civic or digital process map should make visible:
1. channel boundaries;
2. routing responsibility;
3. current state;
4. applicable clock;
5. closure condition;
6. provenance;
7. correction path;
8. whether AI is optional or required;
9. whether the feedback system itself creates friction;
10. where human decision authority remains.

## Boundary

This case study does not establish that any named institution has a closure problem. It records an experience and preserves questions for future research.

> Preserve the possibility before deciding what it is.
