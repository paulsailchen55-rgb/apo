# Archival Workflow

## Purpose

This document applies the standard APO archival workflow to the California Adaptive Dome & Yurt Housing Pathways project.

The archive should preserve the development of the idea, not merely its latest summary. It should retain context, distinctions, uncertainties, decisions, and the path from conversation to implementation.

## 1. Conversation-Wide Inventory

Before restructuring or expanding the project, identify all distinct bodies of work that emerged:

- Project
- Potential project
- Research question
- Framework or concept
- Article or book idea
- Experiment or prototype
- Supporting idea
- Personal or philosophical exploration

Do not assume every idea is a project. Keep unrelated ideas separate unless the conversation clearly connects them.

## 2. Scope of This Project

The primary project is a practical pathway for people and organizations that have already chosen dome- or yurt-based housing. It is not a persuasion campaign intended to prove that domes are superior.

The project seeks to make development easier through:

- AI-assisted guidance
- Flowcharts
- Databases
- Websites and downloadable HTML tools
- Printable instructions
- Accessible explanations
- Agency contact information
- Research libraries and books
- Professional and technical referral pathways
- County-by-county documentation

## 3. Preserve Distinctions

Every archival note should distinguish, when possible, among:

- User statement or preference
- Personal observation
- Assistant interpretation
- Proposal
- Documented fact
- External evidence
- Legal requirement
- Engineering assumption
- Hypothesis
- Disputed claim
- Unknown or unresolved question

Do not convert a preference, metaphor, hypothesis, or interpretation into an established fact.

## 4. Evidence Labels

Use the following labels:

- **KNOWN:** Supported by reliable documentation or directly verified information.
- **CALCULATED:** Derived through an explicit calculation or model.
- **TESTED:** Evaluated through physical, computational, or procedural testing.
- **PROPOSED:** A suggested design, workflow, or implementation.
- **SPECULATIVE:** A hypothesis requiring further investigation.
- **UNKNOWN:** Not yet established.

## 5. Discussion and Decision Notes

For significant discussions, record:

1. The question being discussed.
2. The user's stated intention.
3. Options considered.
4. Decisions or preferences expressed.
5. Reasons supplied by the user.
6. Assistant-generated interpretations, clearly labeled.
7. Evidence still needed.
8. Open questions and next actions.

Do not silently replace the user's intent with a more conventional framing.

## 6. Project-Specific Intent

The project supports people who have already chosen dome or yurt housing. It should make the pathway as easy as possible regardless of age, disability, technical background, income, or organizational capacity.

Possible users include:

- Individuals and families
- Older adults
- People with disabilities
- Nonprofits
- Cooperatives
- Community organizations
- Developers
- Dome or yurt communities

The project may examine single dwellings, tiny structures, large homes, connected structures, and community-scale arrangements.

## 7. Regulatory and Technical Caution

The tool must distinguish guidance from approval. It should identify applicable authorities, current sources, permit requirements, professional review needs, and unresolved jurisdiction-specific questions.

California requirements may vary by jurisdiction and may change over time. Record the source, URL, date checked, applicable jurisdiction, and relevant code edition whenever possible.

## 8. Historical and Cultural Material

Historical and cultural material should be preserved respectfully and specifically. Identify the community, region, source type, and degree of certainty when discussing Indigenous architecture or related traditions.

Do not generalize across all Indigenous communities. Label modern hypotheses about archaeological features as speculative unless supported by reliable evidence.

## 9. Upgrade Discipline

Each upgrade should:

- Preserve existing work unless a change is intentional.
- Add genuinely relevant context or artifacts.
- Record the date and purpose of the upgrade.
- Update the changelog.
- Preserve unresolved questions rather than forcing premature conclusions.
- Avoid deleting or merging folders without explicit approval.

## 10. Provenance Chain

Where practical, preserve this chain:

original conversation → archival interpretation → human revision → external research → prototype or implementation

The archive should make it possible to tell what originated with the user, what was generated or organized by AI, and what was later verified or changed.

## Guiding Principle

**Preserve the possibility before deciding what it is.**
