## Identified Bodies of Work

### 1. Community Microleasing / Flexible Space Access

- **Classification:** Framework / potential project.
- **Scope:** Short-duration access to indoor and outdoor spaces, potentially in 45–60 minute blocks and extending toward longer temporary use and conventional leases.
- **Core elements:** Space identification, scheduling, permitted activities, responsibility, declarations, records, cleanup, safety, and renewal/expiration procedures.
- **Santa Cruz context:** Downtown sidewalk vending, vacant storefront activation, pop-up occupancy, conventional commercial leases, and barriers to temporary access.

### 2. Mobile Food and Music

- **Classification:** Potential project / operational prototype.
- **Scope:** A mobile food-and-drink and acoustic-music service using approved sites and defined operating windows.
- **Existing archive representation:** Extensive prior files covering water, equipment, ingredients, transparency, operations, partnerships, liability, and regulatory questions.
- **Relationship:** A concrete use case that may use flexible space access but remains a separate project.

### 3. Countywide GIS Parking Identification and Flexible Parking Leasing

- **Classification:** Potential project / research direction / technical civic-infrastructure concept.
- **Scope:** GIS-linked identifiers for parking spaces, jurisdictional overlays, authorized temporary agreements, privacy-preserving vehicle association, payment, nonprofit assistance, and non-punitive support workflows.
- **Relationship:** A specialized application of microleasing, kept separate because it requires distinct transportation, property, privacy, data-access, and enforcement research.
