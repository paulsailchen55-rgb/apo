# Meaningful Housing Choice

## Ten questions
1. What would make a housing offer feel like a real choice?
2. How many options can a person reasonably compare at one time?
3. Can the person ask questions before deciding?
4. Are costs and rules explained in plain language?
5. Can the person consider location, transportation, safety, and accessibility?
6. Can personal preferences be respected without being treated as unreasonable?
7. Can the person decline without punishment or loss of support?
8. Is there enough time to make an informed decision?
9. Who can review a decision if the person believes an offer is unsuitable?
10. How could agencies measure whether residents experienced genuine choice?
