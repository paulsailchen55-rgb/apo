# Quickstart

1. Identify the state, county, city, or unincorporated jurisdiction.
2. Identify the property condition: existing house, vacant land, multiple structures, or redevelopment site.
3. Select the configuration: one dome, one yurt, multiple structures, connected community, or existing house plus accessory structures.
4. Research zoning, dwelling classification, setbacks, utilities, access, fire, and permitting.
5. Research seismic, wildfire, wind, flood, landslide, soil, and drainage conditions.
6. Identify agencies, professionals, documents, costs, and next actions.
7. Record sources, dates checked, and unresolved questions.
