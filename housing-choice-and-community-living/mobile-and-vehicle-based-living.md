# Mobile and Vehicle-Based Living

## Ten questions
1. What kinds of mobile or vehicle-based housing are being considered?
2. What basic needs must the arrangement support?
3. Where can people legally and safely stay?
4. How would water, sanitation, electricity, and waste be handled?
5. What insurance and registration requirements apply?
6. How can accessibility and medical needs be supported?
7. What storage and security problems arise?
8. Could people move between supported locations?
9. What community services would remain available while moving?
10. Which laws or policies create the greatest barriers?
