# Participation Modes

Each mode receives its own current-rule profile.

## Phone

Check dial-in number, identification, muting, recognition, time limit, and disconnection procedures. Optimize for voice-only clarity.

## Video / Zoom

Check platform, registration, raise-hand, microphone/camera, chat, screen-sharing, and technical procedures. Optimize for clear framing and readable visual support.

## In-person

Check location, arrival and sign-in requirements, speaker recognition, microphone use, timekeeping, accessibility, and room procedures.

## Written or scheduled meeting

Check submission limits, deadlines, recipient, meeting-request process, and whether the interaction is public or private.

The system must retrieve and cite the applicable official instructions instead of assuming that one mode's rules apply to another.
