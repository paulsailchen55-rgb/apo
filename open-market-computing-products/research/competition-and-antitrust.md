# Competition and Antitrust

## Research question

When do proprietary software, firmware, interfaces, repair restrictions, or technological locks become relevant to competition policy?

## Questions

- How do restrictions affect aftermarket competition?
- When can interoperability restrictions harm competition?
- What is the role of intellectual-property protection?
- What evidence is needed before government intervention?
- Can a parallel open-product requirement or incentive increase competition without prohibiting proprietary products?
