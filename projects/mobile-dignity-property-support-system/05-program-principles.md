# Step 5 — Program Principles

1. **Dignity and autonomy:** Participants retain control over their movement, belongings, choices, and personal information.
2. **Accessibility:** Design for varied bodies, abilities, ages, literacy levels, and living circumstances.
3. **Safety before expansion:** Validate stability, braking, visibility, load limits, and storage safety before adding complexity.
4. **Repairability:** Prefer understandable, replaceable, locally serviceable components and documented maintenance procedures.
5. **Privacy by design:** Collect only necessary information; separate vehicle identification, participant identity, and location data.
6. **Public-benefit access:** Do not make permanent housing, income, or continuous connectivity automatic prerequisites.
7. **Recovery and consent:** Establish clear, consent-based procedures for lost, removed, found, or stored belongings.
8. **Human oversight:** AI may coordinate information and requests but should not independently make high-impact eligibility, exclusion, or property decisions.
9. **Mission protection:** If paid services coexist with public-benefit services, protect free or subsidized capacity through transparent rules and governance.
10. **Continuing consequence awareness:** Regularly examine unintended effects, listen to participants, create low-burden feedback channels, and retain authority to change the system.
