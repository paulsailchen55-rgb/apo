# Adaptable Housing and Reuse

This section considers housing that can change with residents’ needs, remain repairable, and use materials responsibly. The questions are intended to support constructive research rather than prescribe one design.

## Ten questions for further inquiry
1. What physical changes should a home be able to accommodate?
2. How can accessibility be built in or added later?
3. Which components should be modular, repairable, or replaceable?
4. How can residents maintain privacy while sharing adaptable spaces?
5. What costs arise during construction, repair, relocation, and reuse?
6. Which materials are durable, safe, reusable, or locally available?
7. How could a design support different household sizes and life circumstances?
8. What rules would allow residents to modify their homes responsibly?
9. How could adaptable housing be moved or repurposed without displacing residents?
10. What should happen to the materials at the end of the building’s useful life?
