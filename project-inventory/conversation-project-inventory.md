## Deliberate boundaries

The lendable measurement concept is distinct from Minimal Functional Continuity even though both involve devices and resilience. It is also distinct from Personal Agency Infrastructure, which is a general provenance/permissions architecture; the new project applies related principles to physical measurement lending.

The health and environmental sides should remain separate data pathways. The environmental path should not automatically feed a clinical chart, and the health path should not automatically expose personal medical information to public agencies.

The library lending model and neighborhood tool-shed model are alternatives, not one project. The library route is being explored because it appears to have an existing public lending infrastructure; this is a research hypothesis requiring local verification.

Privacy concerns about Wi-Fi, surveillance, FLoC-like data collection, vehicle/audio/location inference, hacking, and re-identification are preserved as threat-model concerns. They are not converted into claims that a particular library, utility, manufacturer, or other institution is presently conducting such surveillance.

“Anonymized” is retained as a user concept but treated as an unresolved technical/legal question rather than a guarantee. Device provenance can be preserved separately from borrower identity in principle; current device capabilities and legal arrangements remain unresolved.

The property-access body of work remains separate from Vertical Commons, Modular Public Infrastructure, FlexSpace, and Property Risk & Options Map. The Omega Principle remains separate from Endpoint Closure/STUR. Ocean technical research remains separate from public communication and private philosophical provenance.
