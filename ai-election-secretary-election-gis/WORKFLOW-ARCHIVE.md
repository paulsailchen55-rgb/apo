# WORKFLOW ARCHIVE

## 2026-09-24 — Initial architecture

### Trigger
Search for an Android AI note-taking / secretary application led to a broader question: could there be a host/router for many AIs that receives raw material, creates a brief, understands the task, and directs the work to the appropriate AI/tool?

### Architecture shift
The concept changed from selecting one "best AI" to designing an AI Secretary that can orchestrate multiple specialized capabilities.

### Election use case
The user connected this architecture to researching every election decision before voting.

### Initial pipeline
DISCOVERY -> BRIEF -> EVIDENCE -> ADVERSARIAL RESEARCH -> SOURCE COMPARISON -> RELATIONAL MAP -> DECISION TREE -> HUMAN DECISION -> ARCHIVE

### Candidate model
Candidate dossiers replace simple biographies for deep research.

### GIS insight
Candidate information can be represented as layers over a geographic/institutional map, similar conceptually to a GIS.

### Important distinction
VOTABLE BY ME != RELEVANT TO ME

### Three circles
MY BALLOT / MY GOVERNMENT / MY ENVIRONMENT

### Global extension
The project was explicitly expanded to elections worldwide, including single-party systems and political systems where electoral events do not function like U.S. competitive elections.

### Safety / integrity requirement
The system must support human decision-making rather than make political choices for people.

### Evidence discipline
Use:
KNOWN
CALCULATED
TESTED
PROPOSED
SPECULATIVE
UNKNOWN

Add, where useful:
DOCUMENTED CLAIM
CONTESTED
UNVERIFIED
