# Future Additions

This file identifies possible additions and provides a simple way to decide whether each belongs in the archive. Inclusion should preserve provenance and should not require every idea to become a formal project.

## Ten questions for further inquiry
1. What new material is being considered for inclusion?
2. Where did it originate, and when was it created?
3. Is it an observation, research result, proposal, experiment, or interpretation?
4. Does it add substantial information not already present in the folder?
5. Which existing document should it extend or link to?
6. Does it require independent legal, technical, historical, or policy verification?
7. What personal or identifying information should be removed or protected?
8. What uncertainties should be stated alongside it?
9. Would a short note, a dedicated document, or a separate project folder be most appropriate?
10. What useful question could another reader investigate next?
