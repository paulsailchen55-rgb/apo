# Interoperability

## Research question

Can interoperability requirements preserve an open computing market without requiring manufacturers to publish all proprietary source code?

## Questions

- What interfaces can be standardized?
- What information must be documented?
- When can interoperability be required?
- What proprietary interests remain legitimate?
- How do competition laws treat interoperability?
- Could interoperability provide much of the practical benefit of openness?
