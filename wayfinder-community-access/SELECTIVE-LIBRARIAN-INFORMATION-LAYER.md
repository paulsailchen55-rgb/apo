# Selective Librarian Information Layer

WAYFINDER is an information and navigation layer for local community information.

The Selective Librarian concept generalizes one of its central questions:

> How can a person find useful information without having to inspect the entire information field?

For WAYFINDER, the field may contain schedules, locations, eligibility conditions, accessibility information, changes, closures, community events, and source records.

A Selective Librarian could let a person state an explicit need such as:

- “I need a free meal today.”
- “I need a library with computer access.”
- “I want a civic meeting I can attend.”
- “I need a place reachable on foot.”

The system can retrieve relevant records while preserving source and verification context.

The critical boundary remains:

> Information navigation, not information ownership.

The system should not silently rank people, organizations, or communities. It should help the person inspect available information and make their own choice.
