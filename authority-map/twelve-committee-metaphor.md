# Twelve / 4×3 / 6-Pair Committee Metaphor

## Classification

Personal/philosophical hypothesis and organizing metaphor.

## Idea

The conversation explored whether a “twelve” pattern could be used as a conceptual way to think about committees:
- twelve
- four groups of three
- six pairs
- overlapping responsibility
- time/clock and geographic/map layers

## Archive rule

This is **not** presented as a documented rule governing U.S. government committees.

Its possible value is as a design metaphor for distributing responsibility, cross-checking, and making complex authority legible.

## Unresolved

Whether any part of the metaphor corresponds usefully to actual institutional design remains a research question.
