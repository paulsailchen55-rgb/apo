# Three-Hour Research Sprint — Prototype 01 Real-World Case

## Purpose

This is a focused working plan for continuing Prototype 01 using the TMC / NORI Area D case.

The objective is **not** to finish the global-energy-financial-system inquiry in one sitting.

The objective is to move from:

> **One documented case**

toward:

> **One traceable system transformation with enough evidence that another person can inspect the reasoning.**

---

# Hour 1 — Reconstruct the Case Before Interpreting It

## 00:00–00:15 — Establish the timeline

Build a simple dated sequence:

~~~text
2011
→ exploration contract / sponsorship
→ subsequent exploration and resource definition
→ technology development
→ financing events
→ 2025 sponsorship agreement
→ 2025/2026 regulatory activity
→ current state
~~~

For each event record:

- date;
- actor;
- action;
- document;
- evidence category.

Do not interpret yet.

### Output

CASE-TIMELINE.md or a section added to the case archive.

---

Create an actor map:

~~~text
TMC

├── TMC USA
├── technology partners
├── financial/capital providers
└── potential downstream/offtake relationships

NAURU
├── Government

PHYSICAL SYSTEM
├── seabed
~~~

For each actor ask:

- What can this actor provide?
- What does this actor depend on?
- What authority does this actor have?
- What risk can this actor transfer?
- What can this actor exit?

### Output

> **What happened, when, who did it, and what document says so?**

without explaining what it means.

# Hour 2 — Follow the Transformation

## 01:00–01:25 — Capital → capacity

Now investigate the money.

For each major financing event:

~~~text
CAPITAL
↓
INSTRUMENT
↓
RECIPIENT
↓
STATED PURPOSE
↓
WHAT WAS BUILT / PURCHASED / DEVELOPED
↓
NEW CAPACITY
~~~

Do not ask yet whether the financing was "good" or "bad."

Ask:

> **What capability did the money make possible?**

This is the bridge between finance and physical reality.

---

## 01:25–01:50 — Capacity → future obligation

Trace:

~~~text
CURRENT CAPACITY
↓
EXPECTED FUTURE ACTIVITY
↓
EXPECTED PRODUCTION
↓
EXPECTED REVENUE
↓
FINANCIAL / CONTRACTUAL CLAIMS
~~~

Separate:

- actual;
- contracted;
- forecast;
- assumed;
- contingent.

This is where the earlier concept of **interest as a temporal claim** can be tested without assuming that every financial claim functions the same way.

## 01:50–02:00 — Short break / reset

Stop reading.

Write down, from memory:

1. What surprised me?
2. What remains unclear?
3. Where did the model seem to fit?
4. Where did the model fail?

Do not research during this ten-minute interval.

# Hour 3 — Choice, Exit, and the Larger System

## 02:00–02:25 — Build the choice-set map

Compare:

### Before

What choices existed before TMC/NORI entered this resource-development pathway?

### After

What choices exist now?

### Because of the project

Which choices became possible because of:

- exploration;
- technology;
- capital;
- institutional relationships;
- regulatory pathways?

### Because of the project

Which choices became more difficult, costly, specialized, or dependent?

Do not assume the answer is "fewer."

The result could be:

~~~text
MORE CHOICES
FEWER CHOICES
DIFFERENT CHOICES
CHOICES SHIFTED TO OTHER ACTORS
UNKNOWN
~~~

---

## 02:25–02:50 — Perform the Exit Test

For each apparent exit, describe the layers:

~~~text
KNOWN
↓
POSSIBLE
↓
LEGAL
↓
AFFORDABLE
↓
PHYSICALLY FEASIBLE
↓
ORGANIZATIONALLY FEASIBLE
↓
DURABLE
~~~

Example:

> "NORI can formally renounce rights."

Then ask:

> What happens to sunk technical investment?

> What happens to contracts?

> What happens to personnel and specialized equipment?

> What happens to future expectations?

> What happens to other parties?

> What happens to the underlying resource opportunity?

The goal is to determine whether **formal exit equals material exit**.

---

## 02:50–03:00 — Fold back out

Return to the larger map:

~~~text
MINERAL DEMAND
      ↓
RESOURCE DISCOVERY
      ↓
EXPLORATION
      ↓
CAPITAL
      ↓
TECHNOLOGY
      ↓
REGULATORY PATHWAY
      ↓
POSSIBLE EXTRACTION
      ↓
MATERIAL + ENERGY THROUGHPUT
      ↓
REVENUE
      ↓
FINANCIAL CLAIMS
      ↓
NEW RESOURCE / ENERGY DEMAND
      ↺
~~~

Then ask only one question:

> **Did this project solve an existing constraint, move the constraint, create a new dependency, or is it too early to know?**

Possible answers:

- solved;
- moved;
- created;
- mixed;
- unknown.

No overall verdict.

# What We Deliberately Are NOT Doing Tonight

We are not trying to:

- decide whether deep-sea mining should happen;
- calculate an investment rating;
- declare TMC successful or unsuccessful;
- prove the ocean is an extractive frontier;
- prove finance causes extraction;
- prove a civilization-scale energy trap;
- resolve the environmental debate;
- build a complete financial model.

Those can come later.

The purpose of this sprint is simpler:

> **Can we follow one real system from opportunity → entry → investment → capacity → obligation → changed choices → exit → outcome, while keeping the physical, financial, institutional, ecological, and human layers distinct?**

If yes, the framework has something worth building on.

If no, we revise the framework before scaling it.

---

# Guiding Rule for the Three Hours

> **Follow the evidence until the map changes.**

Do not make the evidence fit the map.

Let the case tell us where the map is wrong.
