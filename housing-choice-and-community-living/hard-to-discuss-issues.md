# Hard-to-Discuss Issues

This document preserves difficult questions without presenting them as settled conclusions. The wording is intended to support respectful inquiry, especially where people may have different experiences or interpretations.

## Ten questions for further inquiry
1. When does a limited housing offer become difficult to refuse in practice?
2. How can researchers distinguish administrative limits from disregard for resident agency?
3. What forms of market power or ownership concentration affect available choices?
4. How can people be treated as participants rather than only as cases or eligibility categories?
5. What safeguards help residents raise concerns without risking essential support?
6. How should disagreements between agencies, landlords, and residents be documented?
7. Which claims can be supported by records, and which remain hypotheses?
8. What tradeoffs should be made explicit when resources are limited?
9. How can research include criticism without assigning motives prematurely?
10. What language would allow people with different perspectives to continue the conversation?
