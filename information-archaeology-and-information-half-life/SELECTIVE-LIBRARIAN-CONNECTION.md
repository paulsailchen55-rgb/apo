# Selective Librarian Connection

This project studies how public information can remain technically online while becoming difficult to discover, search, verify, or independently recover.

The **Selective Librarian** concept extends that question from preservation to navigation:

EXISTENCE → ACCESSIBILITY → SEARCHABILITY → DISCOVERABILITY → PRESERVATION → INDEPENDENT RECOVERABILITY

A durable archive is not enough if people cannot find the relevant material.

The Selective Librarian should operate on top of preserved records rather than replacing them. It can help a person locate relevant documents, sections, historical versions, and related records while keeping the original evidence inspectable.

Research question:

> Can selective retrieval improve access to older public information without making the retrieval system itself the new point of disappearance?

Safeguards include preserving original records, provenance and dates; showing why a record was retrieved; allowing broader searching; distinguishing missing records from irrelevant records; and preserving the research path.
