# Immediate Action Model

The interface begins with available time: 15 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour, 3 hours, today, or scheduled later.

Each result should show the action, location, duration, cost, permission status, safety notes, accessibility, verification date, and whether formal registration is required.

Priority factors may include immediacy, time fit, accessibility, clarity, permission, safety, cost, verification freshness, practical benefit, and duplication. These factors should organize options, not rank human worth or judge neighborhoods and organizations.

The system should explicitly support a result of **No verified action found** and suggest safe alternatives such as reporting a need, checking a public resource, or contacting a local group.
