# Current Status

Date of archive: 2026-09-18

The Omega principle is still being clarified in conversation.

The strongest current formulation is not a finished doctrine. It is a design-and-use question:

**When we begin or use something, are we acting in a way that remains compatible with its proper end?**

A second question concerns the maker:

**If we take materials from the earth to make something, have we designed a credible path for those materials toward an appropriate closure at the end of useful life?**

The concept currently contains two related but separable layers:

1. End-oriented conduct: present choices should remain compatible with the intended end.
2. Material closure: making should account for what happens to the material after useful life.

The next stage should clarify definitions and boundaries before attempting to turn the concept into a formal project, technical standard, or public proposal.
