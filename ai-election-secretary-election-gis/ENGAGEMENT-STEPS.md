# ENGAGEMENT STEPS

## Near-term

1. Establish the exact November 3, 2026 ballot for the user's Santa Cruz jurisdiction.
2. Separate current officeholders from offices actually up for election.
3. Build the master Election GIS index.
4. Create one research record per candidate and measure.
5. Verify every major claim against primary or otherwise identified sources.
6. Build the three-circle map: My Ballot / My Government / My Environment.
7. Prototype candidate relational maps.
8. Prototype measure interaction maps.
9. Test AI routing on real election research tasks.
10. Preserve all outputs in Markdown and maintain provenance.

## Medium-term

- Define a portable data schema for candidates, offices, measures, jurisdictions, evidence, sources, claims, and relationships.
- Define a model/tool routing interface.
- Define privacy and retention rules.
- Define publication approval gates.
- Test multiple independent research agents against the same question.
- Develop a visual Election GIS prototype.

## Long-term

- Compare election systems across countries.
- Develop country-specific political-system adapters.
- Study single-party and dominant-party electoral contexts.
- Study the global consequences of civic AI assistants.
- Explore public-interest / open implementations.
