# Open Food Access & No-Line Pantry

## Status

**PROPOSED / ARCHIVE-STAGE** — This folder preserves the concept and begins staged software planning. It is not a deployed food-access service.

## Purpose

A free and open-source, privacy-minimal food-access platform for food pantries, community gardens, and other nonprofit food programs.

The system should allow a seeker to:

- register once with a participating organization when required;
- use ordinary SMS, phone assistance, or optional web access without downloading an app;
- view available food and choose suitable items where the organization permits;
- communicate allergies, dietary restrictions, preferences, and food-quality concerns;
- receive an appointment or pickup instruction instead of waiting in an unnecessary line;
- request information for today only, or optionally receive temporary seasonal and harvest notifications;
- select a city, neighborhood, garden, crop, or food category for area-specific calendar information;
- optionally export or subscribe to predicted harvest and distribution events through iCalendar-compatible tools.

The system should allow nonprofits to:

- publish available inventory;
- manage requests, preparation, and pickup windows;
- support same-day harvest and distribution workflows;
- publish verified harvest confirmations separately from forecasts;
- record recurring seasonal, pantry, and store-food distribution patterns without profiling individual seekers;
- minimize collection of personal data;
- connect through documented APIs/adapters to existing operational software when useful;
- operate through self-hosted or community-hosted infrastructure without mandatory proprietary platform dependence.

## Harvest calendar and temporary signals

`HARVEST-CALENDAR-AND-TEMPORARY-SIGNALS.md` documents a proposed city-by-city harvest intelligence layer. It distinguishes ecological observations, forecasts, local inspection, and confirmed food availability. It also describes calendar exports, area-specific selection, today-only requests, temporary notification permissions, expiration, and privacy boundaries.

## Core understandings

`CORE-UNDERSTANDINGS.md` preserves developing ethical and educational ideas about seasonal food knowledge, appreciation, human dignity, children's connection to land, and recurring food-access patterns. These are recorded as observations, interpretations, and design hypotheses—not as universal findings or requirements.

## Separate pathway

This folder also documents a separate **Daily Harvest & Biblical Foodway** pathway. Its spiritual interpretation is preserved in `BIBLICAL-FOODWAY-INTERPRETATION.md` and should not be silently converted into secular claims or universal operating rules.

## Evidence labels

- **KNOWN:** User-described experience, stated design requirements, or documented source material.
- **PROPOSED:** Design ideas not yet implemented or validated.
- **CALCULATED:** Derived technical or operational estimates.
- **TESTED:** Verified through an actual prototype or trial.
- **SPECULATIVE:** Possibilities requiring research.
- **UNKNOWN:** Unresolved questions.

## Guardrails

- Do not delete or merge existing APO material without explicit approval.
- Preserve user observations, interpretations, hypotheses, and proposals as distinct from verified facts.
- Do not claim that the platform guarantees food safety, privacy from telecom providers, or universal eligibility.
- Do not expose private-property plant locations without authorization or encourage trespass.
- Do not treat forecast dates as guarantees of readiness, safety, or availability.
- Design for people with basic phones, limited data, limited literacy, language differences, and changing circumstances.
