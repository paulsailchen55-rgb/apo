# FlexSpace Santa Cruz — Archive Package

This folder preserves a working Civic Flex-Space concept and the context developed around it in conversation.

## Status

Working concept / reference package. It is not presented here as a completed policy, legal instrument, zoning amendment, engineering specification, or validated implementation.

## Contents

- `what-i-mean-by-civic-flex-space.md` — one-page briefing prepared for discussion with Workbench and City/County stakeholders.
- `relationship-responsibility-context.md` — conceptual context for the Responsibility portion of the framework, including the user's covenant/relationship analogy and its secular translation.

## Archive note

The material distinguishes proposals, interpretations, and questions from established facts. Specific legal, zoning, building-code, insurance, accessibility, liability, and administrative mechanisms remain subjects for qualified review.

The broader FlexSpace idea is larger than the Bright Plaza/1214 Soquel Avenue application. The Bright Plaza proposal is treated here as a concrete experiment through which parts of the broader architecture could potentially be examined.

## Core mnemonic

**Space × Time × Use × Responsibility (STUR)**

The acronym is retained as a working mnemonic discovered during the development of the concept. It is not presented as an established technical standard.
