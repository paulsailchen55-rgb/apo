## Project Concept

The project explores whether libraries could lend standardized, easy-to-use devices for temporary home and environmental measurement. Potential areas include:

- Indoor air quality
- Particulate matter, including PM2.5 and PM10
- Carbon dioxide
- Temperature and humidity
- Mold-related environmental indicators
- Other home-environment measurements
- Optional health-related measurements, subject to appropriate privacy, safety, and clinical limitations

The broader concept is intended to serve ordinary residents, renters, homeowners, apartment residents, and people living in HUD-assisted housing.

## Important Design Principles

- Use devices that are understandable and require minimal training.
- Prefer validated or clearly documented measurement tools.
- Give borrowers control over their records and how information is shared.
- Distinguish environmental measurements from medical diagnosis.
- Consider privacy, data provenance, accessibility, maintenance, calibration, and safe handling.
- Explore whether measurements could optionally support healthcare records, environmental records, voluntary research, or authorized agency processes without automatically transferring ownership or interpretive authority away from the borrower.
