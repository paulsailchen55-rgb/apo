## Boundaries preserved

Technical recovery hypotheses (pulverization/blasting, automatic vibration layering, microbial sealing, unrestricted AI processing control, and residential deployment) remain unresolved.

User observations about property, landlords, network providers, government processes, or community conditions are not converted into established facts.

Metaphors, philosophical principles, and analogies are not treated as scientific or legal evidence.

Related projects remain separate where their research questions differ, even when they share themes of closure, modularity, access, resilience, or agency.

This audit is an archive record, not a claim that every listed item is complete, validated, fundable, legally established, or ready for implementation.
