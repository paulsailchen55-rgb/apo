# Housing Choice, Community Living, and Property Access

## Purpose

This folder preserves exploratory research about housing access, homelessness, meaningful choice, community living, property structures, and alternatives to conventional housing arrangements.

The archive contains lived experience, research questions, conceptual proposals, and issues requiring further verification. It does not treat every idea as a completed project.

## Core themes

- Meaningful housing choice versus a single available offer
- Housing placement, market structure, and practical constraints
- Mobile and vehicle-based living
- Collective property networks
- Legal title and access to property
- Adaptable, reusable, and community-oriented housing
- Civic processes, documentation, privacy, and accountability

## Provenance

Some material originated in a separate conversation and is identified as such. Later additions should preserve their source and distinguish observations from hypotheses and externally verified claims.

## Scope limitation

This is an evolving research archive, not legal advice, a policy position, or a finalized implementation plan.
